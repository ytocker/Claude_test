# AURORA STAG — Round 1 (5 candidates)

Legendary spectacle ANIMALS skin: a hooved land mammal (the flex), a noble
deer head/forebody crowned by a vast antler-rack rendered as flowing
northern-lights ribbons with floating white star-points. No live particles —
the aurora glow + star-points are BAKED into all 4 frames; the "flap" (no
wings) is reinterpreted as the rack rippling like aurora curtains, with a
small ear-flick on the up-pose.

Shared contract: 64×84 SRCALPHA, body centred at (32,44), head near (44,34),
rack reaches up into the top ~24px. Palette: #1E2A3A body / #3DF2C0 green /
#7A5CFF violet / #FF6BC2 pink / #FFFFFF stars. All variants keep the body mass
pinned at the collision centre so the tall rack never drags the body off.

Sheet: `docs/animals/aurora_stag/round_1.png` — hero 130px, 40px smooth, and
the honest 40px NEAREST x3 (level + dive) on a night sky.

---

## v1 · SPECTRUM CROWN — the textbook legendary
- **What:** symmetric 3-tine branching stag rack from each skull root, with a
  forward brow-tine; full-spectrum gradient (green→violet→pink) cycling up the
  ribbons, a star at every fork/tip plus scattered star-dust between the beams.
  Dark #1E2A3A body, regal almond eye + stern brow.
- **40px tell:** a recognizable branching antler crown that glows multicolour —
  reads instantly as "deer made of aurora."
- **Ripple:** the gradient phase shifts and tine tips wobble per frame; ears
  flick on the up-pose.
- **Weak spots:** the most "expected" silhouette of the five; at 40px the dense
  tines can crowd into a single glowing mass — may need fewer/bolder tines for
  cleaner separation.

## v2 · TWIN CURTAINS — soft drapery
- **What:** two tall translucent aurora CURTAINS instead of branchy antlers —
  layered green-over-violet sheets with vertical light-rays and a wavy hem,
  sweeping up and apart; sparse bright stars on the crest, gentle round eye.
- **40px tell:** two glowing veils rising from the head — the most authentically
  "northern lights" of the set.
- **Ripple:** the sheets billow left/right per frame (true curtain motion).
- **Weak spots:** least "antler-like" — risks reading as flames/smoke rather
  than a rack at 40px; the translucency may wash out against a bright-day sky
  (needs a day-backdrop check). Silhouette is softer/less iconic than a rack.

## v3 · WIDE RACK — sunset-aurora trophy
- **What:** a very wide, low-spread, many-tined rack (the big-game-trophy
  shape) in a warm PINK/GOLD aurora scheme (the rarer "fire-sky" aurora) over a
  lighter dusky-blue body; warm haze glow behind the whole crown.
- **40px tell:** sheer width — the rack spans far past the head, unmistakable
  even tiny; the pink/gold reads differently from every other skin in the store.
- **Ripple:** gradient shimmer + tip wobble; ears flick up-pose.
- **Weak spots:** width may clip the 64px canvas edges on dive-rotation; warm
  palette is the furthest from the brief's cool aurora — confirm it still reads
  as "aurora" and not "fire/phoenix" overlap.

## v4 · LYRE BEAMS — boldest silhouette
- **What:** two big curving lyre-shaped main beams sweeping out, up, then back
  inward to nearly touch (minimal branching for maximum silhouette), one short
  inner tine each, a big crowning star at each tip; violet→green ribbons, very
  dark body, fierce expression.
- **40px tell:** two clean glowing arcs + two bright tip-stars — the highest-
  contrast, most legible read at gameplay size; survives motion best.
- **Ripple:** beam shimmer wobble per frame; ears flick.
- **Weak spots:** arguably reads more "horns/lyre" than "antlers" — less of a
  stag rack; may feel too minimal for a *legendary* (could want one more tine).

## v5 · CONSTELLATION — celestial / ethereal
- **What:** the rack drawn as a STAR-CONSTELLATION lattice — bright star-nodes
  joined by thin glowing aurora connector lines (join-the-dots antlers). Full
  spectrum, high star density, gentle eye; a faint violet halo unifies the crown.
- **40px tell:** a cluster of bright stars above the head — the "antlers are
  literally made of stars" concept; freshest idea of the five.
- **Ripple:** nodes drift/breathe per frame so the constellation shimmers.
- **Weak spots:** thinnest lines + smallest features → most at-risk in the 40px
  NEAREST read (lattice can dissolve into loose dots); needs the connector lines
  thickened or nodes enlarged to hold the antler shape at gameplay size.

### Cross-cutting open questions for the art-director
- Which silhouette family wins: **branchy rack** (v1/v3), **curtain** (v2),
  **bold arcs** (v4), or **constellation** (v5)?
- Cool full-spectrum vs the warm pink/gold (v3) — does warm stray too close to
  the existing Phoenix flex?
- Star density: dense (v1/v5) vs sparse-but-bright (v2/v4) for a cleaner crown.
- Body darkness: dark #1E2A3A (v1/v2/v4/v5) makes the aurora pop hardest; the
  dusky body (v3) softens contrast — keep the body dark?
