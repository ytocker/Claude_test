# Skybit Animals — Expansion Brainstorm (9 → 20)

**Goal:** 16 fresh creature-skin concepts to grow the ANIMALS store category from 9
to 20+. Skew late-game / aspirational. Exactly **4 LEGENDARY** spectacle skins; the
rest **late-game**. Deliberately out-of-the-box — at least 5 are non-bird / non-winged.

**Buildability ground rules baked into every concept:** purely cosmetic, drawn 100%
from code on a 64×84 SRCALPHA canvas, body mass centred near (32,44), animated over
4 wing poses + dive/climb tilt. Each must READ AT 40px IN MOTION — one bold silhouette
+ one high-contrast signature tell. The flap is reinterpreted (leap, pulse, fin-stroke,
ear-flap, ink-jet, glide-membrane) — physics never changes.

**Already shipping (do not duplicate):** bee, owl, toucan, penguin, bat, flamingo,
bald eagle, dragon, phoenix.

---

## Non-winged / unexpected count

7 of the 16 are non-bird / non-winged (Axolotl, Pufferfish, Jellyfish, Sugar Glider,
Mantis Shrimp, Flying Fish, Cosmic Jelly) — comfortably past the 5 minimum.

## The 16 Concepts

### 1. AXOLOTL — `skin_axolotl`
- **Tier:** late-game
- **Silhouette:** stubby pink amphibian with a fan of six feathery external gills
- **Signature feature:** the gill-frill halo + permanent dot-eyed smile — nothing else in the roster has a "headdress"
- **How the flap reads:** the gill fronds sweep back on the down-pose and bloom open on the up-pose, like a frilled fin-stroke through water
- **Palette:** `#FFC8DD` body / `#FF7AA8` gill cores / `#5A2A3E` smile+eye dots / `#FFE8F0` belly highlight
- **Why it's fresh:** an internet-darling "shouldn't fly" amphibian; the smiling face is pure casual-arcade charm
- **Reference:** axolotl (feathery gills, perpetual smile)

### 2. PUFFERFISH — `skin_pufferfish`
- **Tier:** late-game
- **Silhouette:** near-perfect spiky sphere with tiny fins and a pouty face
- **Signature feature:** the radial spike-halo silhouette — instantly a ball of spikes at 40px
- **How the flap reads:** inflates bigger + spikes flare on the down-pose, deflates slightly on the up-pose (a comedic pulse)
- **Palette:** `#F6C453` body / `#C98A1E` spikes / `#3A2A12` eye+lips / `#FFF1C9` belly
- **Why it's fresh:** the circular spiky shape is unlike any current animal; built-in visual gag
- **Reference:** pufferfish / porcupinefish inflation

### 3. JELLYFISH — `skin_jellyfish`
- **Tier:** late-game
- **Silhouette:** translucent dome bell trailing a curtain of wavy tentacles
- **Signature feature:** the glowing bell-dome with a ring of dangling tendrils — a lantern shape
- **How the flap reads:** bell contracts (squashes) on the down-pose and billows open on the up-pose — a true jelly pulse-jet
- **Palette:** `#B8E1FF` bell / `#7AB8FF` rim / `#E6C2FF` tentacles / `#FFFFFF` core glow (soft inner glow, non-legendary tier)
- **Why it's fresh:** a non-winged pulse animation reads as movement better than any wing; serene + alien
- **Reference:** moon jelly / Atolla jellyfish

### 4. SUGAR GLIDER — `skin_sugar_glider`
- **Tier:** late-game
- **Silhouette:** wide flat kite — limbs spread, patagium membrane stretched corner to corner
- **Signature feature:** the dark dorsal stripe down a stretched flying-membrane + huge round night-eyes
- **How the flap reads:** membrane is taut/wide on the down-pose (gliding) and limbs tuck in on the up-pose (mid-leap) — a glide cycle, not a flap
- **Palette:** `#C9CDD6` fur / `#5A5F6B` dorsal stripe / `#2A2D33` eye mask / `#FFF7E6` belly / `#1A1B1F` big pupils
- **Why it's fresh:** a mammal that glides (no true wings) — the kite silhouette is brand new to the set
- **Reference:** sugar glider / flying squirrel patagium

### 5. MANTIS SHRIMP — `skin_mantis_shrimp`
- **Tier:** late-game
- **Silhouette:** segmented armoured torpedo with raised boxing-club forelimbs and stalk-eyes
- **Signature feature:** technicolor banded carapace + two periscope eye-stalks — a kaleidoscope in armour
- **How the flap reads:** the club-arms cock back on the down-pose and punch forward on the up-pose — a strike, not a flap
- **Palette:** `#2EC4B6` carapace / `#FF6B35` club-arms / `#FFD23F` banding / `#3A1E5A` eye-stalks / `#E0F7F4` highlight
- **Why it's fresh:** the most colour-saturated, alien-bodied pick; the punch animation is unique
- **Reference:** peacock mantis shrimp (16-cone colour, club strike)

### 6. FLYING FISH — `skin_flying_fish`
- **Tier:** late-game
- **Silhouette:** sleek silver dart with oversized pectoral "wing-fins" spread wide
- **Signature feature:** glassy iridescent pectoral fins held like glider wings off a chrome body
- **How the flap reads:** wing-fins angle down + tail-fin kicks on the down-pose, fins flatten to a glide on the up-pose — a sea-skim
- **Palette:** `#9FD8EF` body top / `#E8F4FA` silver belly / `#5AA9D6` fin membrane / `#2A4A5E` eye / iridescent rim sheen
- **Why it's fresh:** literal "a fish that flies" gag rewards the joke; chrome iridescence pops on day skies
- **Reference:** flying fish / Exocoetidae gliding

### 7. CHAMELEON — `skin_chameleon`
- **Tier:** late-game
- **Silhouette:** curled-tail lizard with a tall head-casque and independent turret eyes
- **Signature feature:** spiral-coiled prehensile tail + one swivelled cone-eye — a comic, characterful read
- **How the flap reads:** body shifts through a colour gradient as it tilts (mood-shift shimmer); tongue flicks out on the up-pose like a flap accent
- **Palette:** `#5BC85B` base / `#FF5CA8` flush band / `#3FA0E0` cool band / `#FFD23F` crest / `#234D23` eye-turret
- **Why it's fresh:** colour-shift gimmick is a cheap procedural win; coiled-tail silhouette is unmistakable
- **Reference:** panther chameleon (living-sunset colour shift)

### 8. RED PANDA — `skin_red_panda`
- **Tier:** late-game
- **Silhouette:** round russet fluffball with a fat ringed tail curling up behind
- **Signature feature:** the bold cream-and-rust ringed tail arc + white face-mask — pure cute-factor
- **How the flap reads:** the big tail sweeps up on the down-pose for "lift", paws tuck on the up-pose — a leap-and-balance
- **Palette:** `#C1440E` fur / `#7A2A0C` tail rings / `#FFF4E6` face mask+belly / `#3A1A0C` eyes+nose
- **Why it's fresh:** maximum mascot charm; the ringed tail is a strong, simple silhouette accent
- **Reference:** red panda (ringed tail, face mask)

### 9. HUMMINGBIRD — `skin_hummingbird`
- **Tier:** late-game
- **Silhouette:** tiny teardrop body with a needle bill and a blurred wing-arc
- **Signature feature:** the iridescent gorget throat-patch flashing magenta/green + motion-blur wing crescents
- **How the flap reads:** wings render as a figure-8 blur disc that shifts opacity across the 4 poses — true hover-buzz (distinct from the bee's stripes)
- **Palette:** `#1FA67A` back / `#E8338A` gorget / `#0E5C44` wing blur / `#FFF7E6` belly / `#2A2A2A` bill
- **Why it's fresh:** a bird, but the hover-blur + gorget flash is a different tell from anything in the set
- **Reference:** ruby-throated hummingbird (gorget, hover)

### 10. SEAHORSE — `skin_seahorse`
- **Tier:** late-game
- **Silhouette:** vertical S-curve body with a horse-head crest and a coiled tail
- **Signature feature:** the upright question-mark profile with a tubular snout — a one-of-a-kind vertical silhouette
- **How the flap reads:** the tiny dorsal fin flutters fast (shimmer ripple) while the tail uncoils slightly on the up-pose — a hover-drift
- **Palette:** `#FF9E2C` body / `#C96A12` ridge bumps / `#FFD98A` snout / `#5A2E0C` eye / `#FFE8C2` highlight
- **Why it's fresh:** the vertical S-shape breaks the horizontal-body convention; instantly readable oddity
- **Reference:** seahorse (upright S-curve, dorsal flutter)

### 11. THUNDERBIRD — `skin_thunderbird` *(LEGENDARY)*
- **Tier:** legendary
- **Silhouette:** broad-winged raptor wreathed in jagged storm-cloud feathers
- **Signature feature:** crackling lightning-fork accents arcing off the wingtips + glowing storm-blue eyes
- **How the flap reads:** wings throw forked lightning on the down-stroke (animated zap flicker), cloud-feathers compress on the up-pose — a thunderclap beat
- **Palette:** `#3A4A6B` plumage / `#1A2238` storm shadow / `#FFE14D` lightning / `#7FD0FF` electric rim / `#FFFFFF` flash core. **Glow:** pulsing electric-blue body aura + intermittent lightning particle flicker
- **Why it's fresh:** a culture-rich legendary distinct from dragon/phoenix; lightning particles = baked spectacle
- **Reference:** Native American Thunderbird (storm-bringer)

### 12. CELESTIAL KITSUNE — `skin_kitsune` *(LEGENDARY)*
- **Tier:** legendary
- **Silhouette:** nimble fox body fanned by a spread of nine glowing tails
- **Signature feature:** the nine-tail fan trailing wisp-fire + a glowing forehead mark — an unmistakable tail-burst
- **How the flap reads:** the nine tails sweep and flare across the 4 poses (the "wings" are the tail-fan), each leaving a brief foxfire trail
- **Palette:** `#FFF4D6` fur / `#FF7A1A` ear+tail tips / `#FFFFFF` blaze mark / `#B86BFF` foxfire wisps / `#FFD24D` aura. **Glow:** warm gold body glow + violet foxfire particle wisps off the tails
- **Why it's fresh:** a non-bird legendary — the flying "wing" is a tail-fan; mythic prestige with cute fox charm
- **Reference:** Kyūbi no Kitsune (nine-tailed Inari fox)

### 13. AURORA STAG — `skin_aurora_stag` *(LEGENDARY)*
- **Tier:** legendary
- **Silhouette:** noble deer head/forebody crowned by a vast branching antler-rack
- **Signature feature:** the antlers rendered as flowing aurora ribbons with floating star-points — a glowing crown
- **How the flap reads:** the aurora antlers ripple like northern-lights curtains across the poses; small ear-flicks accent the up-pose (no wings — the antlers carry the motion)
- **Palette:** `#1E2A3A` body / `#3DF2C0` aurora green / `#7A5CFF` aurora violet / `#FF6BC2` aurora pink / `#FFFFFF` star-points. **Glow:** soft aurora-gradient antler glow + drifting star particles
- **Why it's fresh:** a hooved land mammal as a legendary flex; the aurora antlers are the showpiece tell
- **Reference:** stag mythology + aurora borealis colour language

### 14. COSMIC JELLY — `skin_cosmic_jelly` *(LEGENDARY)*
- **Tier:** legendary
- **Silhouette:** a galaxy-filled translucent bell trailing star-streamer tentacles
- **Signature feature:** a swirling nebula inside the dome + constellation-dot tentacles — a jellyfish made of space
- **How the flap reads:** the bell pulses (squash/billow) and the internal nebula slowly rotates; tentacles trail stardust on each pulse
- **Palette:** `#1A0A33` void / `#7A3CFF` nebula violet / `#3CC8FF` nebula cyan / `#FF6BD0` nebula pink / `#FFFFFF` stars. **Glow:** inner nebula glow + drifting stardust particle trail
- **Why it's fresh:** fuses the non-winged jelly pulse with cosmic spectacle — the only "deep-space animal" in the set
- **Reference:** deep-sea bioluminescent jelly + galaxy/nebula imagery

### 15. QUETZAL — `skin_quetzal`
- **Tier:** late-game
- **Silhouette:** crested bird with two impossibly long ribbon tail-streamers
- **Signature feature:** the trailing emerald tail-ribbons + crimson breast + spiky head-crest
- **How the flap reads:** wings beat normally while the long tail-streamers undulate behind across all 4 poses — flowing ribbon motion
- **Palette:** `#1FB36B` plumage / `#0E6B3E` tail shadow / `#E03A3A` breast / `#FFD23F` crest accent / `#0A3A26` eye
- **Why it's fresh:** the ribbon-tail is a graceful new silhouette; sacred-bird glamour without going legendary
- **Reference:** resplendent quetzal (Quetzalcoatl lineage)

### 16. GRIFFIN — `skin_griffin`
- **Tier:** late-game
- **Silhouette:** eagle-headed forebody fused to a tawny lion hindquarter with a tufted tail
- **Signature feature:** the two-tone split — feathered eagle front / furred lion rear + hooked beak — a chimera tell
- **How the flap reads:** broad eagle wings beat in a powerful raptor cadence; the lion tail-tuft flicks on the up-pose
- **Palette:** `#E8C24A` lion fur / `#8A5A1E` wing feathers / `#F4E4B8` head feathers / `#3A2A12` beak+eye / `#C9A23A` rim
- **Why it's fresh:** a classic mythic hybrid the set lacks; the front/back material split is a strong, simple read
- **Reference:** Greek griffin (eagle + lion)

## RANKING (strongest first)

1. **CELESTIAL KITSUNE** *(legendary)* — best overall + best legendary showpiece. A
   non-bird legendary whose nine-tail fan IS the flight animation; mythic prestige,
   cute charm, and foxfire spectacle in one unmistakable tail-burst silhouette.
2. **AXOLOTL** *(late-game)* — top non-legendary. Internet-beloved, instantly charming,
   the gill-frill halo + smile is a 40px slam-dunk and nothing else has a "headdress."
3. **AURORA STAG** *(legendary)* — a hooved mammal as a flex; aurora-ribbon antlers are
   a gorgeous, novel showpiece tell distinct from fire/phoenix glow.
4. **JELLYFISH** *(late-game)* — the pulse animation reads as motion better than wings;
   serene, alien, and a clean lantern silhouette. Gateway for the Cosmic Jelly upsell.
5. **MANTIS SHRIMP** *(late-game)* — the most colour-saturated, alien pick; the punch
   animation and banded armour are unique and bold at 40px.
6. **THUNDERBIRD** *(legendary)* — culture-rich storm legendary; lightning particles are
   easy procedural spectacle and clearly distinct from the dragon.
7. **SUGAR GLIDER** *(late-game)* — fresh kite silhouette, a true non-winged glider,
   maximal night-sky cute-factor with the big eyes.
8. **PUFFERFISH** *(late-game)* — strong circular spiky silhouette with a built-in
   inflate gag; very legible.
9. **COSMIC JELLY** *(legendary)* — gorgeous nebula-in-a-bell, but overlaps thematically
   with the plain Jellyfish; ranked behind the other three legendaries for distinctness.
10. **RED PANDA** *(late-game)* — pure mascot charm; ringed tail is a clean accent.
11. **CHAMELEON** *(late-game)* — colour-shift is a cheap procedural win and the coiled
    tail is unmistakable.
12. **GRIFFIN** *(late-game)* — classic mythic hybrid; the front/back material split
    reads well, though it's a more familiar fantasy beat.
13. **QUETZAL** *(late-game)* — elegant ribbon-tail, but risks reading "just a fancy
    bird" next to toucan/flamingo.
14. **FLYING FISH** *(late-game)* — fun joke and chrome sheen, but a quieter silhouette.
15. **SEAHORSE** *(late-game)* — the vertical S-curve is novel but the small, delicate
    body is the hardest 40px read in the set.
16. **HUMMINGBIRD** *(late-game)* — lovely but the closest in spirit to the existing
    bee (small + hover-blur); kept for the gorget flash, ranked last for overlap risk.

### Headline picks
- **Strongest overall:** CELESTIAL KITSUNE
- **Best legendary showpiece:** CELESTIAL KITSUNE (with AURORA STAG the runner-up flex)
- **Strongest non-legendary:** AXOLOTL
