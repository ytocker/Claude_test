# FLYING TOASTER (`skin_toaster`) — Round 1

Secret ultra-premium ABSURD flyer: the player's flapping bird becomes a chrome
toaster with little wings — the After Dark gag. Five genuinely different takes,
all sharing the same bold silhouette (a rounded appliance box) and the same
40px tell: **chrome/metal body + two gold toast slices jutting from the top
slot + little flapping wings.** Each animates the wings over the 4 base poses
and bobs the toast up/down (mid-pop) so it reads alive.

Reference: the 1991 Berkeley Systems "Flying Toasters" — a classic 2-slot
chrome toaster with **white feathered bird wings**, a side lever, and gold
toast. v1 is the faithful homage; the others push finish, toast count, wing
style, and a face for variety.

Review sheet: `docs/animals/toaster/round_1.png` — hero 130px + 40px NEAREST
x3 (level / dive) on DAY and NIGHT skies (the honest gameplay read).

---

## V1 · After Dark Classic (chrome)
- **Look:** mirror-chrome rounded 2-slot toaster, white feathered bird wings,
  two gold toast slices mid-pop, chrome side lever + round dial, chrome feet.
  Pure homage, no face.
- **40px tell:** chrome box (bright-top / dark-belly metal shading) + two gold
  slices + the white feather wing-span breaking the silhouette.
- **Day/night:** white wings + gold toast carry both; the chrome reads as cool
  grey on day, catches the night gradient at night.
- **Weak spots:** chrome is the lowest-saturation body of the five, so it leans
  hardest on the wings + toast for the colour pop; the lever/dial detail is
  near-invisible at 40px (fine — they're hero-scale flavour).

## V2 · Cream Retro Diner (face)
- **Look:** 1950s cream enamel body on a chrome plinth, teal pinstripe, chrome
  lever, soft angel wings, two buttered toast, and a chubby googly-eyed face.
- **40px tell:** warm-cream box + gold toast + the two big eyes (the face adds
  a second high-contrast read on top of the toast).
- **Day/night:** cream is bright and warm — strong on the night sky, slightly
  softer against a pale day sky (the chrome plinth + dark eyes anchor it).
- **Weak spots:** the teal pinstripe is a hero-only grace note; risk of the
  face + toast competing — kept the eyes low and central so the toast owns the
  top edge.

## V3 · Copper Steampunk (1 toast)
- **Look:** hammered-copper body, brass slot trim + lever + riveted dial, a
  SINGLE tall toast popping HIGH (caught mid-pop) with tiny steam, bronze
  feathered wings.
- **40px tell:** rich copper box + one tall gold slice spiking above the body
  + brass glints. The single tall slice is the most distinct silhouette of the
  set.
- **Day/night:** warm copper + brass is the richest metal on a night sky;
  still reads against day because the gold slice + dark slot give contrast.
- **Weak spots:** copper and the gold toast are close in hue — the darker
  crust rim + the bright slice bevel are what keep the slice separate from the
  body; watch they don't merge further at the smallest scale.

## V4 · Mint Kawaii (face)
- **Look:** pastel-mint appliance, white base trim, big shiny googly eyes,
  rosy cheeks, fluffy white angel wings, two buttered toast. Max-cute.
- **40px tell:** soft mint box + gold toast + the oversized shiny eyes.
- **Day/night:** mint is mid-bright — pops nicely on the dark night sky;
  against a similar-value day sky it leans on the dark eyes + white wings +
  gold toast for separation.
- **Weak spots:** lowest-contrast body against a bright/teal day sky of the
  five; the big eyes + white trim + outline are doing the heavy lifting there.

## V5 · Noir Chrome (modern)
- **Look:** sleek matte-black + chrome modern toaster, chrome base rail + top
  cap, a glowing orange slot (hot element), two gold toast, sharp swept
  steel-feather wings, an LED dial dot. Premium, minimal.
- **40px tell:** black-and-chrome two-tone box + the glowing gold slot/toast.
  The black body is the boldest silhouette against a DAY sky; the chrome + the
  glow carry it at NIGHT.
- **Day/night:** best dual-target of the five — dark body for day, bright
  chrome + ember glow for night.
- **Weak spots:** the steel wings are sharper/less "bird" than v1's feathers —
  reads more aero-gadget than After Dark; the glow line is thin (risk of
  washing out at the very smallest scale).

---

### Recommendation framing for the art-director
- Most faithful to the gag: **v1**.
- Most charming / "no way" personality: **v2 / v4** (faces).
- Most premium-feeling for an ultra-premium secret: **v3 (copper)** and
  **v5 (noir chrome)**.
- Best day-AND-night robustness: **v5**, then **v1**.
