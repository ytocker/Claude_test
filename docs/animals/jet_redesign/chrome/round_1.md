# Secret JET FIGHTER redesign — RETRO CHROME · Round 1

Concept: a polished BARE-METAL Cold-War jet (F-86 Sabre / MiG-15 vibe).
Signature is shiny aluminium read VALUE-first — a bright top-highlight band
over a dark belly — plus ONE bold accent per take. Five genuinely different
sub-takes (accent scheme, wing sweep, canopy size, chrome-render method),
NOT five tweaks of one idea.

Sheet: `docs/animals/jet_redesign/chrome/round_1.png`

## What every take shares (the chrome read)

- VALUE-driven metal: `_AL_HI` hot top band → `_AL_BODY` mid → `_AL_LO` dark
  belly → `_AL_EDGE` panel/outline shadow. This is what makes the polished
  aluminium hold at 40px on both skies rather than dying to one flat grey.
- Baked afterburner pulse reused verbatim from the production jet (`_pulse` /
  `_pitch` / `_baked_flame` / `_glow`), peak-capped to the rear so the metal
  nose stays the dominant read. Cool canopy dot = constant anchor vs the warm
  burner.
- Drawn nose-RIGHT, upright, level; the sheet applies the production 205°
  spin so the reviewer sees the true in-game inverted nose-up attitude.

## The five takes

- **V1 · SABRE RED-NOSE** — swept wing USAF Sabre. RED radome nose +
  red/white CHECKERED tail band. Chrome = hard top-highlight band over dark
  belly. Medium bubble canopy. Twin burner. Accent: red.
- **V2 · MIG RED-STAR** — STRAIGHT (un-swept) wing MiG-15, stubby barrel
  fuselage, single high tail + single centre nozzle. RED air-intake nose
  ring + a bold RED STAR on the mid-fuselage. Chrome = panel-line FACETS
  (dense seams) not a smooth band. Small canopy + wingtip fuel pods.
- **V3 · BLUE-ANGEL TRIM** — swept wing, BLUE spine stripe edged YELLOW down
  the chrome top band, yellow fin flashes, blue wing leading-edge flash. The
  BIG bubble canopy take. Accent: blue + yellow.
- **V4 · RACING No.7** — raw mirror-bright aluminium air-racer, NO national
  markings. Deep-swept (45°) aggressive wing, a bold black "7" roundel on
  each wing root, black anti-glare panel ahead of the canopy. Accent: black
  number roundel (red-rimmed).
- **V5 · GOLD-BAR SABRE** — swept Sabre rendered as a rivet-line PANEL GRID
  (faceted chrome), with a single bold YELLOW recognition band, black-
  outlined Korean-War style, wrapped round the rear fuselage. Red wingtip
  caps. Accent: yellow band.

## Coverage of the brief axes

- Accent scheme: red nose+checker (V1), red star on silver MiG (V2),
  blue/yellow trim (V3), raw aluminium + bold racing number (V4), yellow
  recognition band (V5).
- Wing: swept (V1/V3/V5), straight (V2), deep-swept 45° (V4).
- Canopy: medium (V1/V5), small (V2/V4), big bubble (V3).
- Chrome render: hard top-highlight band + dark belly (V1/V3/V4) vs
  panel-line facets (V2/V5).
