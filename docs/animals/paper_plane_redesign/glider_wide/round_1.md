# PAPER PLANE redesign — WIDE GLIDER — Round 1

Concept: a broad FLAT-WING paper glider (wide delta / "Harrier" fold), a
deliberately DIFFERENT silhouette from the production dollar-bill DART. The dart
is a narrow forward triangle; the glider is a WIDE planform reading as a slow
soaring craft. Same skin contract (64×84, mass centre (32,44), nose points
RIGHT, no wings → bank/flutter + soaring pitch sway, baked 1px self-rim).

Sheet: `round_1.png` — each take at hero 130px, then the honest 40px NEAREST×3
gameplay read (level + dive) on BOTH a day sky and a night sky. The CURRENT
dollar dart leads the grid for an apples-to-apples "different silhouette" check.

## The 5 takes (all wide, all nose-right, all hard lit/shadow fold + self-rim)

- **V1 · WIDE DELTA** — archetypal broad triangle, trailing edge spanning nearly
  the full width, blunt-ish nose, single centre keel splitting a bright white
  lit half from a cool-grey shadow half. White printer paper.
- **V2 · SQUARE HARRIER** — near-square stubby box planform, almost-straight
  trailing edge, wide blunt nose, a raised central fuselage STRIP. Warm manila.
- **V3 · SWEPT GLIDER** — strongly raked leading edges sweeping back to a wide
  trailing span; sharper nose but still wide; an inner swept sub-crease. Pale
  blue construction paper.
- **V4 · WINGLET DELTA** — the V1 wide delta with UP-FOLDED wingtips: two small
  dark vertical fins at the trailing corners for the cleanest tip read so the
  span never blurs into sky. White.
- **V5 · KEEL GLIDER** — wide flat wing shelves flanking a BOLD raised central
  keel/fuselage ridge drawn as its own lit-top vs shadow-side facet; the spine
  is the hero. Manila.

## Exploration axes (per the brief)

- wing breadth + sweep: wide delta (V1/V4) vs square Harrier (V2) vs swept (V3)
- nose bluntness: blunt (V1/V2/V5) vs sharper raked (V3)
- centre keel / fuselage strip: thin keel line (V1/V3/V4) vs raised strip (V2)
  vs bold dual-facet ridge (V5)
- wingtip up-fold / winglets: V4
- paper colour: white (V1/V4), manila (V2/V5), pale blue (V3)

## Read check

All five hold a wide silhouette at 40px NEAREST on day AND night via the
hard top-facet vs shadow-facet split + baked self-rim. Did NOT self-judge which
wins — that is the art-director's call.
