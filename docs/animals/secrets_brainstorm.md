# Secret ultra-premium NON-CREATURE flyers — concept brief

Four "???" secret skins sit at the top of the store. Two are locked:
**PAPER PLANE** + **JET FIGHTER**. This brief proposes candidates for the
**other two**, ranges across two veins (man-made flying machines / absurd
"shouldn't-fly" objects), and names a top-2.

## Fixed constraints every concept respects

- 100% procedural, drawn on a **64×84** canvas, body mass ~**(32,44)**.
- **4 wing-pose frames** + dive/climb tilt. The "flap" is reinterpreted per
  object (bank/wobble, afterburner pulse, rotor spin, sail billow, hop).
- **Must read at 40px in motion**: ONE bold silhouette + ONE high-contrast tell.
- Physics never changes — purely cosmetic.

## Pairing logic

Paper plane = humble/papercraft. Jet = sleek/military hardware. The other two
should NOT add a third "aircraft" of the same energy. Best contrast comes from
(a) one more *machine* with a totally different silhouette family (round/dome,
not a pointy fuselage) and (b) one *pure absurd object* for the "no way" laugh.

## CANDIDATES

### 1. UFO  — `skin_ufo`
- **Silhouette:** low silver saucer disc with a glowing glass dome on top.
- **40px tell:** the dome's cyan glow ring + tractor-beam underglow — round,
  unmistakable, opposite of any winged shape.
- **Flap reads as:** the rim of running lights pulses/chases around the disc
  across the 4 frames (no wings at all — the "beat" is a light cycle); a faint
  beam-cone flickers under it.
- **Palette:** `#C7CDD6` brushed silver / `#7A8290` shadow underbelly /
  `#1B2330` dome glass dark / `#4FE9FF` cyan glow / `#B6FF7A` beam-green accent.
- **Why fresh:** the only round-domed silhouette in the whole roster; pure
  sci-fi spectacle and reads at any size.
- **Ref:** retro 60s/70s flying-saucer sprite language.

### 2. FLYING TOASTER  — `skin_toaster`
- **Silhouette:** chrome 1940s toaster with little feathered wings, a slice of
  toast popping up.
- **40px tell:** the gold toast jutting from a mirror-chrome box — warm pop on a
  cool metal block, instantly funny.
- **Flap reads as:** the bird-wings literally flap through the 4 poses (the one
  object whose wings are canon), toast bobs up/down with the beat.
- **Palette:** `#E6EAF0` chrome highlight / `#9AA3B2` chrome mid /
  `#4B5566` chrome shadow / `#E9A23B` toast / `#5A3A1E` crust.
- **Why fresh:** the definitive "shouldn't fly" gag — and the only concept where
  real wings ARE the joke, so the flap animation sells itself.
- **Ref:** After Dark "Flying Toasters" screensaver (Jack Eastman, 1989).

### 3. HOT-AIR BALLOON  — `skin_balloon`
- **Silhouette:** fat teardrop envelope above a tiny wicker basket.
- **40px tell:** big bold colour-banded teardrop dome — huge readable shape,
  burner flame as a warm dot.
- **Flap reads as:** burner flame flares bright/tall on the up-beat and dims on
  the down-beat; envelope subtly swells with the pulse.
- **Palette:** `#FF5A4D` red band / `#FFC93C` gold band / `#3AA0FF` blue band /
  `#8A5A2B` basket / `#FFE08A` flame.
- **Why fresh:** the cosy, slow-romantic machine — tonally opposite the jet;
  the colour bands make it pop on day and night skies.
- **Ref:** classic festival balloon banding.

### 4. MAGIC CARPET  — `skin_carpet`
- **Silhouette:** a rippling rectangular rug seen at a 3/4 tilt, tassels
  trailing.
- **40px tell:** the ornate diamond-medallion centre + fringe — a flat woven
  shape, nothing else in the game looks remotely like it.
- **Flap reads as:** the carpet ripples in a wave (front edge lifts, back edge
  follows) across 4 frames; tassels swing — a billow, not a flap.
- **Palette:** `#7A1E2B` deep crimson field / `#E9C24A` gold border /
  `#16414A` teal medallion / `#F2E4C0` cream fringe / `#C8893B` accent.
- **Why fresh:** pure Arabian-Nights fantasy among hard machines; the rippling
  cloth animation is unique and gorgeous.
- **Ref:** Aladdin / Magic Carpet (Bullfrog) flying-rug language.

### 5. ROCKET  — `skin_rocket`
- **Silhouette:** stubby cartoon rocket with three fins and a porthole.
- **40px tell:** the long orange-blue afterburner flame trailing below the
  bullet body.
- **Flap reads as:** the exhaust flame surges long/bright then chuffs short
  across the 4 frames (thrust pulses); fins steady.
- **Palette:** `#E64B4B` red nose / `#EDEFF2` white body / `#2C3340` shadow /
  `#FFB23E` flame core / `#FF5E3A` flame tip.
- **Why fresh:** vertical-thrust energy, retro tin-toy charm — but risks reading
  too close to the JET's "fast pointy machine + exhaust" family.
- **Ref:** Tintin / tin-toy rocket.

### 6. RUBBER DUCKY  — `skin_duck`
- **Silhouette:** glossy bath-toy duck — round body, flat base, stub tail.
- **40px tell:** the saturated yellow blob + orange bill highlight; the squeak
  hole as a tiny dark dot.
- **Flap reads as:** stubby cartoon wing-nubs paddle, and the whole body
  squishes/bounces (squash-and-stretch) with the beat — a buoyant hop.
- **Palette:** `#FFD21E` yellow body / `#E5A600` yellow shadow /
  `#FF7A1A` bill / `#FFFFFF` eye-shine / `#3A2A12` eye dot.
- **Why fresh:** the most "aww" of the set; high-saturation yellow screams off
  any sky. But it edges toward "creature," softening the non-creature theme.
- **Ref:** classic bath rubber duck / Sega-blue-sky toy palette.

### 7. SHOPPING CART  — `skin_cart`
- **Silhouette:** wire-basket cart on wheels, handle bar at the back.
- **40px tell:** the criss-cross wire mesh basket — a see-through grid, totally
  alien to every solid-bodied skin.
- **Flap reads as:** front wheels spin and the cart tilts nose-up/down (a
  bronco-buck) across 4 frames; a stray plastic bag flutters off the handle.
- **Palette:** `#D7DCE2` chrome wire / `#8C93A0` wire shadow /
  `#E23B3B` red handle grip / `#F4F6F8` highlight / `#5A6270` deep shadow.
- **Why fresh:** maximum absurdity, peak "no way" — but the open wire grid is
  the riskiest 40px read (mesh can mush); needs bold strokes.
- **Ref:** runaway-cart internet meme energy.

### 8. WITCH'S BROOMSTICK  — `skin_broom`
- **Silhouette:** a slender stick with a flared bristle bundle, riderless,
  trailing a little starry sparkle.
- **40px tell:** the fan of straw bristles + a sparkle trail — long, diagonal,
  reads as a streak in motion.
- **Flap reads as:** bristles fan wider/sweep on the beat and the sparkle trail
  puffs (baked particle dots vary across 4 frames); a tilt-bob like it's
  kicking off.
- **Palette:** `#6B4A22` handle wood / `#C79A4E` straw / `#8A6A2E` straw shadow
  / `#9A6BFF` magic sparkle / `#F0E2FF` sparkle highlight.
- **Why fresh:** Halloween-flavoured magic that's *very* not-a-vehicle and not-a
  -creature; the sparkle trail is cheap spectacle. Slimmest silhouette of the
  set, so it leans on the trail to stay readable.
- **Ref:** witch broom / Kiki's Delivery Service.

## RANKING

1. **UFO** — cleanest non-creature 40px read in the set; the domed disc is a
   silhouette family nothing else owns, and the light-cycle "flap" is pure
   premium spectacle. Reads day or night.
2. **FLYING TOASTER** — the funniest, most iconic "shouldn't fly" gag; its real
   wings make the 4-pose flap effortless and on-theme, and chrome+toast pops at
   40px. Maximum surprise for a top-tier secret.
3. **MAGIC CARPET** — gorgeous unique rippling-cloth animation, strong fantasy
   contrast to the machines; slightly more detail-dependent at 40px.
4. **HOT-AIR BALLOON** — bold readable dome and cosy tone, but a "calm machine"
   is less of a jaw-drop for an ultra-premium secret.
5. **RUBBER DUCKY** — adorable and saturated, but reads as a creature, blurring
   the non-creature hook.
6. **BROOMSTICK** — great theme, leans hard on the sparkle trail to survive
   40px (thin silhouette).
7. **ROCKET** — charming but too adjacent to the JET (pointy body + exhaust).
8. **SHOPPING CART** — peak absurdity, but the wire-mesh 40px read is the
   shakiest; high build risk.

## TOP 2 PICKS (to sit beside PAPER PLANE + JET FIGHTER)

- **UFO (`skin_ufo`)** — the *machine* slot, but a round-domed alien craft that
  shares zero silhouette with the paper plane or jet. Cleanest read, biggest
  "premium" glow spectacle.
- **FLYING TOASTER (`skin_toaster`)** — the *absurd object* slot, the definitive
  "no way" reveal, with wings built into the gag so the flap animation lands for
  free.

Together the four read as: humble paper craft → sleek war machine → alien
saucer → kitchen appliance with wings. Maximum spread, four unmistakable
silhouettes, two genuine laughs.
