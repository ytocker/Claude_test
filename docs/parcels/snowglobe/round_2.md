# SNOWGLOBE — round 2

PREMIUM / legendary-secret tier parcel: a collectible miniature universe — a
glass dome holding a tiny swirling cosmos on a WIDE gold pedestal.

Round 1 verdict was ITERATE: gorgeous in isolation but at carry scale it read
as a purple gem with a gold underline — the dome/base stack was occluded, the
day glow was absent, and the glass tells lived in the occluded top. Round 2
re-weights every identity cue into the lower/visible half.

## What changed (all five critique notes)

1. **Wide flat gold PEDESTAL with a hard dark SEAM.** The foot now flares to
   `bot_hw = 18` (clearly wider than the `orb_r = 13` dome) with a `top_hw = 13`
   top and a taller `base_h = 8`, so the visible lower half reads "round thing
   sitting ON a stand". A 2px near-black SEAM (`#0C081C`) sits where the glass
   meets the foot, with a bright sheen lip just under it — the dome now reads as
   a separate object on the pedestal even with the top occluded. This is the
   strongest non-occluded cue and it carries the read.
2. **Baked DAY inner glow.** The interior is no longer a flat violet disc: a
   small off-centre cool-white specular HOTSPOT (`#F4F0FF`, lower-left of the
   visible arc) plus a brighter violet CORE (`#7A5CD6`) give the day orb real
   internal light and focal pop against the blue sky — independent of the night
   additive halo, which still blooms on top at night.
3. **One strong glass-highlight CRESCENT on the LOWER-LEFT dome.** The catch-
   light arc moved from the occluded top to the visible lower-left (190°→255°),
   the cheapest "glass, not a solid ball" cue, now placed where Pip never covers
   it. It survives the 22px down-scale.
4. **Star dots cut to 3 bigger, higher-contrast specks**, all in the central +
   LOWER visible zone (radii 2/2/1, each with a faint twinkle ring). The 1px
   scatter that read as noise is gone.
5. **Wide DAY value gap.** The cosmos now ramps dark sphere RIM (`#24184C`) →
   mid violet (`#3A2A6E`) → bright lit CORE (`#7A5CD6`), biased toward the lower
   half, so the orb reads volumetric — a lit ball, not a flat violet disc.

KEPT: the hero silhouette, tilt survival (dome stays radially dominant across
−25/0/30/60/90°), and the night additive violet bloom — none touched.

## Carry confirmation (on the crops)

- **Gold pedestal carries the read.** On the DAY carry crop and on the on-red
  (Pip-body proxy) crop, the wide flat gold base + the hard dark seam read as a
  pedestal from the visible lower half alone — even with the top of the dome
  occluded it is unmistakably an orb ON a stand, not a gem with an underline.
- **DAY orb now has internal light.** The day crop shows the cool-white
  specular hotspot and the lit violet core over a dark rim — the sphere has a
  clear focal pop and reads volumetric against the blue sky without the night
  halo. At true 22px (day / night / on-red row) all three contexts read as a
  glowing orb on a gold pedestal; the violet interior and warm gold both stay
  well clear of Pip's red.
