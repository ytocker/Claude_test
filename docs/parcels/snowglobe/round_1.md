# SNOWGLOBE — round 1

PREMIUM / legendary-secret tier parcel: a collectible miniature universe — a
glass dome holding a tiny swirling cosmos on a short gold pedestal.

## 22px read
Orb-on-a-pedestal: a glowing DOME/SPHERE sitting on a stubby gold trapezoid
BASE. The sphere-plus-foot is the whole read. The dome dominates the silhouette,
the gold foot anchors it, and a thin dark glass outline holds the round shape
against both skies. Reads as a globe at 22px without relying on interior detail.

## Palette
- DAY — glass dome `#CFE3F2` over a deep-violet interior `#3A2A6E` (lit upper
  `#5B469E`); gold base `#E8B23C` (sheen `#FBDF8E`, shade `#B08322`); warm star
  dots `#FFF4D0`; dark glass outline `#16102E`.
- NIGHT — a BAKED INNER NEBULA radial gradient `#6A4FD0` core → `#C77FE8` edge
  filling the dome, with bright star specks and a cool crescent rim highlight
  `#EAF2FF`. A soft additive violet halo blooms under the dome.

## Night glow
The nebula gradient is baked into the interior and a soft additive halo is laid
UNDER the dome, so the contained cosmos is a faint violet aura in daylight and
BLOOMS against the dark night sky (see GAMEPLAY — NIGHT). It clearly emits light
at night, distinct from the comet's open energy and the lantern's solid warm
glow — this is a contained, cool, miniature cosmos.

## Tilt survival
The dome is a full sphere, so the silhouette is radially symmetric — it reads as
a globe at −25/0/30/60/90°, even inverted, because the orb has no "up". The gold
foot rotates with it but the dome stays the dominant cue at every bank. Verified
across the DAY, NIGHT, and GRAYSCALE tilt rows.

## Carry occlusion
Pip's red body occludes the TOP of the dome. Identity lives in the LOWER/visible
half: the glowing violet cosmos + the gold base. The deep-violet interior and
warm gold are both far from red on the colour wheel, so the parcel separates
cleanly from Pip (see the 4× zoom — gold foot + violet orb read below his body).

## 22px risk
The crescent rim highlight and the secondary glass tint are thin (2px / 1px at
2× before the down-scale) and could soften after smoothscale — they currently
survive, but they are the most fragile cue for the "glass vs solid ball"
distinction. Star dots are kept central/lower and inside the rim so rotation
never strands one on the glass edge.
