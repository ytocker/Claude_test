# BURLAP SACK — LOW tier — round 2

Round 1 verdict was ITERATE: the silhouette concept worked, but at 22px it
read as a coconut/ball (the cinch disappeared) and at 60–90° it elongated
into a gourd. Round 2 attacks both, point by point.

## What changed

1. **Unmistakable waist at 22px.** The onion profile now pinches HARD: the
   neck half-width dropped (4→3) and the early width ramp uses a steep
   exponent so the cinch snaps in fast, carving a real WAIST into the
   outline instead of a stub on a sphere. The tie band is now a WIDE dark
   NOTCH (fattened `OUTLINE` rect + darker `CORD` inner, two cord wraps)
   that survives the downscale as a hard horizontal groove. The neck was
   also lowered into the body so the cinch cuts the silhouette rather than
   perching on top of a ball.

2. **High-tilt read fixed.** The old tall gathered-cloth fan + nub were
   replaced by a single COMPACT rounded knot (a short fat circle sitting on
   the band, never a stem). Because there is no vertical detail to stretch,
   the 60°/90° frames keep a bulb-plus-small-knot read. Validated on the
   grayscale 90° frame — it reads as a tied bulb with a knot off one side,
   not a gourd.

3. **Burlap without folds.** The two literal pucker-fold lines are gone.
   Instead a single faint horizontal WEAVE band crosses the mid-belly (two
   thin value lines, one darker / one lighter, masked to the body), which
   suggests woven cloth and breaks the smooth ceramic/coconut sphere — no
   fine stitching.

4. **Tamed centre highlight.** The centred vertical bright stripe (read as a
   seam/zipper) is replaced by a diffuse, OFFSET soft-cloth bulge: a faded
   ellipse glow on the upper-left belly, so light reads as catching soft
   fabric rather than a seam.

5. **Knot definition.** The compact knot carries an outline ring, a tan
   fill, a tiny top-left highlight, and a short central shadow groove — a
   readable bump+shadow that hints "tied" even at day scale.

## Kept
- The bottom-bulging onion silhouette (the only organic shape in the tier) —
  now a rounder belly (gentler exponent past the waist) so it isn't a sharp
  diamond equator.
- The even baked outline + top-lit tan→base gradient weight.
- The warm `#E8C98A` day/night keyline so the brown body survives the NIGHT
  sky.

## Confirmation
- **22px DAY + NIGHT:** the dark notch + waist read clearly; body shows the
  weave band; knot is a distinct compact bump. Reads as a tied sack, not a
  coconut.
- **Tilt row (−25 / 0 / 30 / 60 / 90°):** all frames hold a tied-sack read;
  the 90° frame is a bulb with a knot off one side — no gourd stem.
- **Grayscale row:** value contrast (dark outline / dark notch / tan body /
  weave band) holds without colour.
