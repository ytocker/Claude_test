# BALLOON BASKET — Round 2

Round 1 verdict: ITERATE. Basket + tilt were KEEP, but the red-striped dome
merged into Pip's red body (red-on-red), sat too high/small, and read as a
bauble — the basket won. This round gets the dome OFF red, grows it, and
drops it so the canopy dominates the carry silhouette.

## What changed
1. **Killed the red-on-red merge (priority 1).** The canopy is now
   **teal/azure + cream** stripes (`STRIPE_TEAL #26A8AE` lit→`#187C86`
   shaded, alternating with cream). Cool teal snaps off Pip's warm red body
   instantly while still reading "candy". The keyline was re-hued to a dark
   teal `#18282C` so it stays a continuous 2px outline all around the dome,
   inboard side included.
2. **Grew the dome ~28% and lowered it (priority 2).** Canopy radii went
   `16×15 → 20×19` and the dome centre dropped `cy 17 → 21`, so the lower
   two-thirds of the canopy sits clear of Pip's belly. The dome — not the
   basket — is now the dominant clear shape below the bird.
3. **Night value rescue.** Added a thin warm arc (`#FADE96`) along the dome's
   lower rim so the cool teal doesn't sink into the dark purple night sky.
4. **Grew the basket ~1px** (`13×11 → 14×12`) with the dome so the box still
   balances the larger canopy, and widened the cords/skirt to match.

KEEP carried through unchanged in spirit: the wicker basket, the strong
tilt-row read, the short incidental cords, the cream crown sheen + skirt.

## Carry-crop read (the verdict)
- **DAY carry (zoom + gameplay-day):** the teal/cream striped dome sits
  clearly below and slightly forward of Pip's red belly — total separation,
  no red-on-red. The big striped round canopy dominates; the wicker basket
  reads as the box hanging under it. It reads as a balloon-over-basket, not
  a bauble.
- **NIGHT carry (gameplay-night):** mid-teal holds its value against the dark
  purple sky; the cream stripes stay bright and the warm lower-rim arc keeps
  the dome bottom from sinking. The glyph stays a balloon at 22px.

## Tilt survival (−25 / 0 / 30 / 60 / 90°)
- The grown round dome silhouette is rotation-invariant; the dome/basket
  pairing reads at every bank across DAY, NIGHT, and GRAYSCALE rows. In
  grayscale the teal and cream now sit at clearly different values, so the
  candy alternation survives value-only.

## 22px notes
- Teal/cream alternation reads cleaner than the old red/cream because the
  two hues are further apart in both hue and value — less risk of muddying
  to a single tone under the smoothscale.
- At extreme bank the basket can still tuck behind the dome's lower edge, but
  the round-cap-over-box read holds.
