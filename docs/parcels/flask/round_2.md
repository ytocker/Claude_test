# GENIE FLASK — Round 2 (HIGH tier)

Round 1 verdict was ITERATE: at carry scale it read as a round blue
potion/gumball, the interior facet seams washed out, the gold collar merged
into Pip's red body + gold goggles, and the silhouette was too close to the
round message-bottle sibling. Round 2 puts the jewel in the OUTLINE and the
gold in a bold band weighted to the lower-visible carry half.

## What changed
1. **Jewel is now in the SILHOUETTE.** The round teardrop is gone. The body is
   an angular briolette: HARD flat shoulder corners up top, straight tapering
   girdle walls, and a chiselled chevron POINT at the base. Even as a flat fill
   the outline says "cut gem" — confirmed on the grayscale silhouette, which is
   instantly an angular pointed gem, not a round vessel.
2. **Bold gold COLLAR band.** Widened from a thin shoulder cap into a wide
   horizontal gold bar across the lower-visible half (waist line, below the
   occlusion line), ringed with the dark sapphire keyline on BOTH edges plus a
   bright top sheen + stud highlights. In the carry crop it separates cleanly
   from a red body and from warm gold goggles.
3. **Day value DOWN / saturation UP.** Core sapphire deepened to `#1E4FC0`
   (from `#3A6FD8`) with a darker `#143386` shaded plane, so the gem out-values
   the day sky by a wide margin. The icy `#DCEBFF` glint is now a single tight
   angular wedge on the upper-left facet, not a soft blob.
4. **Distinct from the message bottle in SHAPE.** Angular pointed gem vs the
   bottle's round horizontal lozenge — a grayscale silhouette separates the two
   siblings at a glance.

## Carry-scale read (the verdict)
- **Angular gem, not a round potion:** YES. The carry crop shows the bold gold
  band over a faceted blue chevron point — hard angular edges, no round blob.
  The chiselled point with its central spine + two value-plane seams reads as a
  cut base in the bottom ~40% that actually clears Pip.
- **Collar separates from Pip:** YES. The both-edge dark keyline rings the band
  so the gold neither bleeds into red body nor reads as a continuation of the
  gold goggles; the dark ring is the separator.

## Structure
The faceted point + gold band are weighted to the LOWER half of the sprite
(band at y≈28/44, point base at y≈42/44) so the carry crop — which only clears
the bottom ~40% — keeps the primary tells. The short neck + domed stopper crown
the top where Pip's body crops in.

## Day / night / tilt
- DAY: deep sapphire holds strong value contrast against the bright sky.
- NIGHT: the masked inner glow still blooms low in the gem so it reads as lit
  on the dark sky; the gold band and dark keyline keep the silhouette.
- TILT ROW (−25/0/30/60/90°): the gem is vertically symmetric about its axis,
  so the angular outline + gold band survive every bank angle; at 90° the gem
  becomes a sideways cut with the band anchoring its midline.
