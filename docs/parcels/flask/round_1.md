# GENIE FLASK — Round 1 (HIGH tier)

A faceted teardrop genie/perfume flask: a wide-bottomed sapphire DIAMOND body
tapering to a narrow shoulder, capped by a gold COLLAR band and a round domed
gold STOPPER on a short neck — a gem-on-a-stem silhouette.

## 22px read
The wide rounded base + tapering top gives an unmistakable teardrop/diamond
profile that separates it from the round message bottle. The gold collar +
domed stopper crown the top as a clear "perfume flask" tell. Two angular facet
seams fan from the shoulder to the base corners, plus a bright left-facet
specular glint, so even at 22px the body reads as a cut jewel, not a smooth
bottle.

## Palette
- DAY: sapphire glass `#3A6FD8` wall, lighter lit core `#7FA8F2`, gold collar/
  stopper `#E8B23C` (sheen `#FFDD88`, underside `#B08220`), icy highlight
  `#DCEBFF`, dark sapphire outline `#12224A`.
- NIGHT: glass holds; a faint inner glow `#9FC0FF` blooms low in the body so
  the jewel still reads as lit on the dark sky. Gold collar stays warm.
- The only cool-blue jewel tone in HIGH — distinct against warm/round siblings.

## Tilt survival
The body is vertically symmetric about its centre axis, so it reads cleanly
across the full TILT ROW (−25/0/30/60/90°). At 90° the diamond becomes a
sideways gem and the gold collar/stopper still anchor one end — confirmed on
the day, night, and grayscale rows in `round_1.png`. The grayscale row keeps a
strong dark-edged silhouette, confirming value separation independent of hue.

## Carry-occlusion
Pip's red body crops the TOP of the parcel in the carry frame. The design is
bottom-heavy on purpose: the widest, most identifiable faceted blue body sits
in the LOWER/visible half, with the gold collar acting as the warm colour break
where parcel meets Pip's red. The zoom crop confirms the blue jewel + gold
collar stay visible below the bird.

## 22px risk
- The round stopper + collar live near the occluded top, so under heavy crop
  the read leans almost entirely on the faceted blue teardrop — acceptable
  since the body is the primary tell, but worth watching if the carry crop is
  deeper than rendered.
- The two facet seams are 1–2px after downscale; if they wash out, the diamond
  could flatten toward a generic blob — the specular glint and dark outline are
  the backup cues.
