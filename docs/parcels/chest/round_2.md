# TREASURE CHEST — Parcel cosmetic (HIGH tier) — Round 2

Round 1 verdict was ITERATE: read as a banded BARREL, the lock didn't win the
centre, and the band-cross nearly vanished in grayscale. Round 2 addresses
every note.

## What changed from Round 1

1. **Lock now LEADS.** The lock plate is pushed one clear value step brighter
   than the bands — its body is `#F6C852` (vs band `#E8B23C`) and its top bevel
   is near-white `#FFF4C8`, the brightest pixel on the whole sprite. It is also
   larger and squarer (12×12, was 9×11). The eye lands on the lock first; the
   bands now read as support, not as competing stripes.

2. **LID SEAM sells "chest".** The horizontal element is no longer a gold strap
   that read as just another band. It is now a true split line: a thin lit gold
   lip (`#FBE08A`) over a thick dark shadow seam (`#6B4612`, darker than the old
   `#A87A1E`) over an outline line. That single dark line splits the domed lid
   from the body — the cue that converts "barrel" into "chest".

3. **Proportion WIDER + SHORTER.** Body widened 34→38px; dome height dropped
   ~15% (18→15px) and lowered. The chest is now clearly wider than tall — the
   genre-standard horizontal box read, not a tall cylinder.

4. **Survives GRAYSCALE.** The read no longer leans on the gold/brown hue
   difference. The lock's near-white bevel stays the brightest value and the
   dark lid seam + band shadow seams stay the darkest, so the band-cross and the
   lock both register in the grayscale −25/0° tiles (verified in the sheet).

5. **Coin glints dropped → one warm rim-glint** on the lock's top-bevel corner
   (`#FFFFFF`), replacing the three coin dots from round 1.

## Kept from Round 1
- Night gold-pop + lock glow (`#FFD659` warm rim) — still mode-agnostic, no
  separate night build.
- Plain-wood dome TOP so it never melts into Pip's red body in carry.
- 2 vertical bands + 1 horizontal element (now the dark lid seam).
- All identity weighted to the lower/visible half; the occluded top is bare wood.

## 22px read (verdict tiles)
- **Lock leads:** confirmed — the near-white plate is the brightest, squarest,
  most central element on DAY, NIGHT, and GRAYSCALE rows.
- **Lid seam reads (chest not barrel):** confirmed — a readable dark horizontal
  line splits dome from body at carried size and at −25/0° banks.
- **Band-cross survives grayscale:** confirmed — lock bevel = brightest, seams =
  darkest; the cross holds without hue.

It reads as a treasure chest at 22px carried, day and night.
