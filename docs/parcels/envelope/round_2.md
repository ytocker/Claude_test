# SEALED MAILER — round 2

Round 1 verdict: ITERATE — strong and close, but the wax seal was too big and
swallowed the twine X (read as "slab with a red button + spokes" rather than a
tied package), and the 90° edge-on frame was weak. This round applies all five
notes.

## What changed (vs round 1)

1. **Seal shrunk ~28%.** `sr` 5 → 4 (area ≈ 0.64×). It now sits at the knot of
   the X as a small bright dot rather than dominating the centre — all four
   twine arms extend clearly past its rim. It is still the brightest, most
   saturated mark, just no longer centre-dominant.
2. **Twine X reads CONTINUOUS, not spokes.** The full corner-to-corner X is
   drawn FIRST (dark 6px under-stroke + 4px twine core), then the small seal
   lands on top of the crossing point. Because the seal is small and the X
   spans the whole diagonal, the cross reads as one continuous mark with a seal
   at its centre — "an X with a seal at the knot," confirmed at 12× and at
   in-play 22/26px on both skies.
3. **Twine thickened ~1px + one shade darker.** Core width 3 → 4, under-stroke
   5 → 6, and `TWINE` darkened `#6B5430` → `#5C4728`. The load-bearing 1px
   twist highlight is DROPPED (it didn't survive the downscale and softened the
   X). The X is now unambiguous before the seal registers.
4. **Squarer body (+2px slab height).** `BH` 26 → 28 so the corner-to-corner
   diagonals read as a CROSS, not a wide flat bowtie. This also gives the 90°
   edge-on present more vertical mass to hold onto.
5. **Padded-mailer diagonal hatch DROPPED.** At 22px it was micro-noise. The
   padding feel is now baked as the faintest per-row value wobble in the kraft
   gradient (a soft ±value alternation, reads as soft padding, never a pattern),
   protecting the cross + seal + kraft read.
6. **90° frame firmed.** The seal rim is now a 2px dark ring (was 1px) and the
   seal outline ring is `sr + 2`, so the central anchor survives the edge-on
   rotozoom. Grayscale 90° re-checked (below).

## KEEP (carried from round 1, protected)
- Kraft tone `#C8A165` and the dark high-value `OUTLINE` (day read).
- Warm `KEYLINE` rim `#E6C488` (night lifeline — the seal pops hardest there).
- Tilt-row robustness: the X-over-slab does not collapse to a card at angle.

## Reads confirmed
- **DAY 22/26px:** continuous twine X with a small red seal knot; dark outline
  carries the silhouette on `(170,220,245)` sky. Reads as a tied mailer.
- **NIGHT 22/26px:** warm keyline glows, the small saturated seal is the
  brightest mark; X stays continuous.
- **TILT ROW (−25/0/30/60/90°):** the cross + central seal anchor hold at every
  bank; no collapse to a card. At 90° the squarer body + 2px seal rim keep the
  central anchor legible.
- **GRAYSCALE 90°:** the cross and the outlined central seal survive by value
  alone — the frame holds edge-on.

The twine X reads CONTINUOUS (an X with a seal at the knot, not a dot with
spokes), and the 90° edge-on frame holds.
