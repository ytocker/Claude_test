# SEALED MAILER — round 1

LOW-tier parcel cosmetic. A padded kraft mailer tied with a twine cross and a
central red wax seal — "special delivery." Built at 2× (44×44), smoothscaled to
22×22 SRCALPHA. Mode-agnostic, single static surface, rotates with Pip's tilt.

## 22px read
A flat, soft-cornered kraft RECTANGLE (a slab, ~34×26 at 2×, not a volume) with
a single diagonal TWINE CROSS corner-to-corner and a small round red WAX-SEAL
dot dead-centre. The cross-tie + seal-dot IS the read — the eye lands on the red
dot first, the X says "tied package," the kraft tone says "parcel."

## Palette
- DAY kraft body `#C8A165` (gradient `(224,192,138)`→`(162,124,72)`).
- Twine `#6B5430` with a `(150,122,78)` twist highlight.
- Red wax seal `#C13B2E` with a darker rim and a glossy offset highlight.
- Dark high-value OUTLINE `(40,24,14)` baked around the silhouette so it reads
  on the bright `(170,220,245)` day sky.
- Warm KEYLINE rim `#E6C488` inside the outline so the slab glows on the dark
  night sky; the saturated seal dot pops harder there than anywhere.

## Tilt survival (slab-vs-card risk)
The brief's core hazard: a bare flat rectangle reads as "a card" when banking.
Mitigations, verified on the tilt row at −25/0/30/60/90°:
- The twine cross spans the full diagonal and is drawn with its own dark
  under-stroke, so the X stays a hard, legible mark at every angle rather than
  blurring into the kraft.
- The wax-seal dot is dead-centre — the one fixed point of a rotozoom — so it
  never drifts and keeps the eye anchored as the slab spins.
- A faint diagonal padded-mailer hatch keeps the body from reading as a smooth,
  blank card without competing with the cross + seal.
- GRAYSCALE row confirms the read holds by value alone: the dark X and the
  outlined central dot survive without the red.

## 22px risk
- The slab is intentionally the thinnest object in the roster; at extreme bank
  (90°) it presents its short edge and is the smallest read in the set — the
  central seal dot is what rescues it. Worth an art-director eye on whether the
  dot is bold enough at 90°, or whether the slab needs a touch more height.
- Twine highlight (1px) and padded hatch are near the limit of what survives the
  downscale; they're carried as supporting texture, not load-bearing detail.
