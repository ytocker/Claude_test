# TAKEOUT PAIL — Round 3

Round 2 verdict was ITERATE: the wire bail was great (KEEP) but the body read as
a **bucket / paint pail**, not a flat-bottomed Chinese oyster pail. Round 3
reproportions it into a folded-card-stock **BOX** and addresses every note.

## What changed (per the four priority notes)

1. **Reproportioned to a flat-bottomed BOX.** Body shortened ~17% (top 16→18,
   bottom 39→37 at 2×, ~19px tall) and the base WIDENED so the bottom is now
   **~70% of the top width** (base half-width 8→11 vs top 16), up from the old
   ~50%. The bottom edge is flat and broad, so after smoothscale the glyph reads
   square-ish — wider than tall in the carry view — instead of a tall tapered
   bucket. Drop shadow widened to match the broader base.

2. **Single vertical FRONT SEAM** added — one clean 1px value-step crease
   (`SEAM_LO`, one notch darker than the body) running down the centre of the
   front face, so the body reads as a folded sheet of card stock, not a smooth
   turned vessel.

3. **Symmetric centre-notch top REPLACED with ONE bold asymmetric diagonal flap
   fold.** The old inverted-V crimp is gone. The closure is now a single
   overlapping tab that sweeps diagonally from the high LEFT rim down across to
   the low RIGHT, over a shadowed `FLAP_LO` underlap — the classic single
   oyster-pail closure. The leading diagonal edge is keylined and lit so the
   slant is crisp at 22px. The wire bail crosses over it. This asymmetry is what
   sells "folded paper" rather than a turned rim.

4. **Wire bail thinned + brightened.** Wire dropped 4px→**2px@2×** (a true
   1px@22 arc) over a thinner 3px keyline, and its value raised ~20% (`WIRE`
   132→176, `WIRE_HI` 188→214). It now holds as a thin bright arc through the
   45°/60°/90° tilt extremes instead of blobbing into a grey mass.

## Kept

- The tall thin **semicircular wire bail** concept and its dark keyline backing.
- The **wide-top / narrow-base trapezoid** distinctiveness (now a gentle flare
  over a wide flat base rather than a hard pinch).
- The **red top focal cap** and the clean grayscale **value structure**
  (self-lit white paper at night, dark keyline against the bright day sky).

## Read check (round_3.png)

- **HERO / ZOOM:** a flat-bottomed takeout BOX — wide flat base, gently flared
  top, one bold red diagonal closure flap, thin wire bail arching above, faint
  front seam down the middle. No bucket / paint-pail silhouette remains.
- **DAY + NIGHT tilt rows (-25° / 0° / 30° / 60° / 90°):** the squat box body and
  the thin wire arc both stay legible across the bank; the red diagonal cap holds
  as the focal; the white body self-lights on the night swatch and the keyline
  holds on the day swatch.
- **GRAYSCALE row:** silhouette alone still reads as a flat-bottomed pail — wide
  base, flared top, thin arch — confirming the value structure carries it.

Confirmed: reads as a flat-bottomed oyster / takeout BOX (not a bucket) at 22px
on both the day and night swatches.
