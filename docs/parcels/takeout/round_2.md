# TAKEOUT PAIL — Round 2

Round 1 verdict was ITERATE: the handle survived but at 22px the glyph read as
a **handbag** — the inverted taper died, the red top read as a flat clasp band,
and the bail was too stout. Round 2 flips it decisively to an **oyster pail**.

## What changed

1. **Inverted trapezoid pushed HARD.** Top half-width raised 14 → **16** and
   base half-width dropped 10 → **8** (at 2×). The top is now exactly twice the
   width of the base, so the splay-top / pinch-base silhouette is unmistakable
   after smoothscale — no longer a near-rectangular purse body.

2. **Folded TOP is now the hero tell.** The flat red band is gone. The top is
   two overlapping angled **fold-tabs** that rise above the rim and meet at a
   centre peak, leaving a wide (5px) **inverted-V notch** break along the top
   edge. The back tab is darkened (FLAP_LO) so the overlap reads as a real fold,
   and the notch/peak edges are keylined so the V break survives downscale.

3. **Handle thinned + heightened to a WIRE bail.** Dropped the stout metallic
   feet + thick crossbar. It is now stubby vertical legs and a full
   near-**semicircular** arch (0 → π) rising clearly above the body, drawn as a
   2px@22 (4px@2×) grey wire over a dark keyline — flimsy wire, not a strap.

4. **Wire-attachment pinch dimples** added at the two top rim corners — small
   dark notches where the bail pinches the paper. Cheap, instantly-readable
   pail cue.

## Kept

- The handle's **dark keyline backing**, so the bail still holds together at
  22px and through the tilt-row banking.
- Clean **day/night value**: the white paper self-lights at night via the lit
  left edge + bright top tab, and the dark keyline holds the silhouette against
  the bright day sky.

## Read check (round_2.png)

- **HERO / ZOOM:** clearly an oyster pail — wide splayed red fold-top with a
  centre notch peak, body pinching to a narrow base, tall thin wire bail arching
  above. No handbag silhouette remains.
- **DAY + NIGHT tilt rows:** across -25° / 0° / 30° / 60° / 90° the wire arch and
  the red inverted-V fold-top stay legible; the body keeps its splay-top /
  narrow-base read; the white body self-lights on the night swatch and the
  keyline holds on the day swatch.
- **GRAYSCALE row:** silhouette alone still reads as pail (taper + arch + fold
  notch), confirming the value structure carries the shape.
