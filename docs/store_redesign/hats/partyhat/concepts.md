# PARTY HAT redesign — 5 concepts

Item: `skin_hat_partyhat` / "PARTY HAT" (group `hats`, cost 220).
Original: teal cone, magenta diagonal stripes, yellow scallop trim, yellow
pom on the tip, chin elastic. A solid but generic kids' party cone.

Five distinct directions, each a recognisable festive headpiece that reads at
a 40px in-motion crop AND as a big store-card product shot. Numbers map to
`design_1 … design_5`.

## design_1 — CONFETTI CONE (elevated classic)
The classic cone, premiumised. Glossy foil-sheen cone (vertical metallic
highlight band), a scatter of multicolor confetti dots across the cone, and
**curly ribbon streamers** spiralling off the tip in place of (or around) the
pom. Keeps the familiar party-cone silhouette so it still reads instantly as
"party hat", but feels celebratory and richer.
Palette: `#FF2E7E` magenta, `#FFD23F` gold, `#19C3C9` teal, `#7B2FF7` violet,
`#FFFFFF` foil highlight.

## design_2 — BIRTHDAY CROWN
A pointed paper birthday crown — gold zig-zag band of 5 triangular points
wrapping the head, each point tipped with a coloured gem (ruby/emerald/
sapphire). Reads as the "it's my birthday" crown. Distinct silhouette: wide
and spiky rather than a tall cone.
Palette: `#F5C542` gold, `#D69A1E` gold-shade, `#E8454A` ruby, `#3FB46B`
emerald, `#3B7BE8` sapphire.

## design_3 — JESTER CAP
A floppy two-point harlequin jester hood draped over the head, alternating
diamond colour blocks (violet/gold), a **jingle bell** dangling off each
drooping point. Playful, asymmetric, instantly different from a cone.
Palette: `#7B2FF7` violet, `#FFD23F` gold, `#19C3C9` teal, `#2A2440` shadow,
`#FFF3C4` bell highlight.

## design_4 — NYE TOP HAT
A glittery mini New-Year's-Eve top hat: short black/midnight-blue cylinder
with a star-spangled band and a gold **star pick** standing on top. The
"countdown" party hat — dressier, sparkly.
Palette: `#16182E` midnight, `#2A2D52` brim-shade, `#FFD23F` gold star,
`#9BB2FF` sparkle, `#FFFFFF` glints.

## design_5 — BALLOON BOUQUET
A little cluster of round party balloons tied with curling strings, bobbing
above the head — red / yellow / teal / violet balloons with a white specular
dot on each and a tied knot + curly string. The "celebration bouquet" hat.
Distinct: rounded multi-blob silhouette, no cone at all.
Palette: `#E8454A` red, `#FFD23F` yellow, `#19C3C9` teal, `#7B2FF7` violet,
`#FFFFFF` specular.
