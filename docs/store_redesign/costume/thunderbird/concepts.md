# THUNDERBIRD — Legendary Skin Concepts

Storm-raptor concepts for the 520-coin legendary. Each layers a head/crest,
a body treatment, wing detail, a talon/foot element, and an atmospheric FX
across the ~64×84 parrot rig (BCX=32 BCY=44, HCX=44 HCY=34), skinned over the
existing 4-pose rotating wing polygon. All buildable from ellipses, polygons,
arcs, lines, and glow blits — no PNGs.

Ranked 1 (best) → 5.

---

## 1. STORM HERALD  — `skin_thunderbird` (living-storm)  · RANK 1

**Hero silhouette.** A hunched, broad-shouldered raptor whose whole body reads
as a walking thundercloud with a bright forked lightning bolt cracking straight
down its chest — the single tell that survives at 40px. Wings are ragged
storm-scud, not clean feathers.

**Objects + placement.**
- *Head/crest:* three back-swept jagged bolt-spikes replacing the crest, tipped
  electric white; a single hot-white glowing eye-slit (the "lightning blink").
- *Body:* dark storm-grey cloud belly built from 3–4 stacked soft-edged ovals,
  darker underneath, with one bold zig-zag lightning bolt polygon down the front.
- *Wings/back:* torn cumulus wing edges (jagged polygon primaries) with a
  crackle of 2–3 thin branching arc-lines that flash brightest on the up-pose.
- *Talons:* claws drawn as tiny forked bolts — glowing arc-line toes gripping a
  spark.
- *Atmosphere:* a soft blue-white glow halo + intermittent spark particles that
  jump between wingtips on the power-flap frame.

**Palette.** `#1B2436` storm-navy · `#3A4A63` cloud-grey · `#8FB4E8` rain-blue ·
`#EAF4FF` bolt-white (glow) · `#FFF6C8` spark-core.

**Distinctness.** The only concept where the *body itself is weather* — a bird
made of cloud and lightning, cold and electric, versus the warm/regal/spectral
others.

## 2. TOTEM THUNDERBIRD  — `skin_thunderbird_totem` (formline)  · RANK 2

**Hero silhouette.** A bold, symmetrical, forward-facing raptor with two curved
horns and thick outstretched wings — the instantly-recognisable Pacific
Northwest formline thunderbird, flattened to poster-graphic blocks that stay
crisp at 40px.

**Objects + placement.**
- *Head/crest:* two upward-curving horns (the thunderbird's formline tell,
  distinct from an eagle); a large red ovoid eye with black pupil.
- *Body:* heavy black formline outline over a red-filled chest with a single
  turquoise U-form "heart" split; clean flat colour blocks, no gradient.
- *Wings/back:* stacked feather-blocks as nested U-forms — black feather with a
  red inner and a turquoise crescent tip, repeated per feather.
- *Talons:* stylised black ovoid feet with red claw crescents.
- *Atmosphere:* restrained — a thin painted lightning-snake motif (zig-zag red
  line) under each wing rather than a glow, keeping it graphic and dry.

**Palette.** `#0E0E12` formline-black · `#C4302B` totem-red · `#1FA6A0`
turquoise · `#F2E8D5` bone-cream (ground). *(No glow — value carried by flat
contrast.)*

**Distinctness.** The only culturally-rooted, flat-graphic, gradient-free
concept — reads as carved crest art, not a glowing storm effect.

## 3. SOLAR WAR CHIEF  — `skin_thunderbird_solar` (solar war chief)  · RANK 3

**Hero silhouette.** A proud, upright golden raptor crowned by a fanned
war-bonnet of upright feathers and backed by a glowing sun-disc shield — a warm
radial burst that reads as "chief" even shrunk down.

**Objects + placement.**
- *Head/crest:* a fanned war-bonnet arc of 5–7 upright polygon feathers,
  gold→orange, each red-tipped, radiating from the crown.
- *Body:* fiery orange-to-gold vertical gradient plumage on the chest with a few
  darker feather-divider lines.
- *Wings/back:* a concentric sun-disc shield behind the shoulders (2–3 nested
  arcs, amber to white-hot centre) so the wings beat *across* the sun.
- *Talons:* bright brass claws gripping a small radiant ember.
- *Atmosphere:* warm gold glow halo + slow rising ember sparks; the sun-disc
  pulses subtly on the flap.

**Palette.** `#7A2E10` ember-brown · `#E8641A` sun-orange · `#FFB021` gold ·
`#FFE9A8` flare-yellow · `#FFF8E0` white-hot (glow).

**Distinctness.** The only warm, radiant, sun-worshipping variant — regal and
ceremonial where Storm Herald is cold and Ancestral is ghostly.

## 4. NIGHT THUNDER  — `skin_thunderbird_night` (night thunder)  · RANK 4

**Hero silhouette.** A sleek, menacing indigo raptor whose dark body is cracked
by pale lightning-scar veins and lit by two glowing violet eyes — a low, sharp,
predatory silhouette with storm-grey ragged wingtips.

**Objects + placement.**
- *Head/crest:* sharp swept-back blade crest; two glowing violet eye-dots as the
  primary 40px tell.
- *Body:* deep indigo-to-violet body with 2–3 pale hairline lightning-scar
  polylines branching across the chest (fixed "cracks", not animated bolts).
- *Wings/back:* violet inner feathers fading to storm-grey ragged tips; a faint
  violet rim-light along the leading edge.
- *Talons:* charcoal claws with a cold violet under-glow.
- *Atmosphere:* dim violet aura + a rare full-scar flash (all crack-lines briefly
  brighten) on the power-flap — a quieter, moodier FX than Storm Herald's
  constant crackle.

**Palette.** `#171029` night-indigo · `#3B2A63` violet-shadow · `#7C5BD6`
electric-violet (glow) · `#5A6472` storm-grey · `#E7DCFF` scar-pale.

**Distinctness.** The only nocturnal, cool-violet, menacing read — dark and
sleek where Solar is bright and Totem is bold-flat; scars are static, not the
live bolts of Storm Herald.

## 5. ANCESTRAL SPIRIT  — `skin_thunderbird_spirit` (ancestral spirit)  · RANK 5

**Hero silhouette.** A translucent, glowing raptor with a soft spectral outline
and ghostly feather-trails streaming behind it — an ethereal shape that reads as
"not quite solid" even at 40px thanks to its bright rim and semi-transparent fill.

**Objects + placement.**
- *Head/crest:* a wispy dissolving crest of thin tapering trails; hollow
  white-glow eyes.
- *Body:* semi-transparent iridescent fill (low-alpha teal→lilac shimmer) with a
  bright spectral rim-line tracing the whole silhouette.
- *Wings/back:* feathers fade to full transparency at the tips; 2–3 curved
  fading trail-arcs stream behind on each flap.
- *Talons:* barely-there glowing claw wisps.
- *Atmosphere:* an animated shimmer gradient cycling across the body + soft
  ghost-particles peeling off the trailing edge.

**Palette.** `#0C1A1E` void-dark (backing) · `#2FA79B` spirit-teal · `#B7A6E8`
spirit-lilac · `#D9FFF6` ghost-white (rim/glow) · shimmer note: cycle rim hue
teal↔lilac each flap.

**Distinctness.** The only transparent/spectral concept — defined by absence and
glow rather than solid colour blocks, so it can't be confused with any of the
four opaque birds.

---

## Ranking rationale

1. **STORM HERALD** — best marries "thunderbird" theme to legendary spectacle
   (live lightning FX) with a body-is-storm silhouette no existing skin shares.
2. **TOTEM THUNDERBIRD** — the most *authentically* thunderbird; bold flat
   formline reads perfectly at 40px and is dirt-cheap to draw crisply.
3. **SOLAR WAR CHIEF** — strongest warm-tier showpiece; sun-disc + war-bonnet
   give real "flex" value.
4. **NIGHT THUNDER** — great mood, but the scar-detail is the riskiest to keep
   readable when small.
5. **ANCESTRAL SPIRIT** — most beautiful in motion, but transparency is the
   hardest to make pop against both bright-day and night skies; ranked last on
   40px-read risk, not on charm.

**Best legendary showpiece:** STORM HERALD (live crackle + spark trails).
**Safest instant-read pick:** TOTEM THUNDERBIRD.
