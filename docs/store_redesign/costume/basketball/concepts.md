# BASKETBALL costume — fresh exploration (5 distinct directions)

**Why this round:** the current scratch basketball build
(`tools/sports_candidates/design_2.py` — purple sleeveless tank + big white
number + brow headband + wristband + high-tops) reads almost identically to the
current soccer build (jersey block + big white number + brow band + footwear).
The two SPORTS must read as obviously different things. So this set diversifies
basketball hard by **style + era + role + silhouette** — not five recolored
tanks. Each must still scream HOOPS at the 40px truth read (day + night), obey
the footprint law (kit over the scarlet body; head stays the macaw; nothing
balloons the torso or drops below the feet line), and the BALL ships separately
as a parcel — the kit alone carries the read.

Anchors (composite space): `HX=47, HY=41, CROWN_Y=31`; body centre
`BCX=32, BCY=52`; feet `(26,65)/(34,65)`; feet-line kit ~`HY+21..27`. Build with
the layered pirate/tennis technique: background elements first, then
fill→shade→outline→highlight per element. Props meant to sit IN FRONT (chain,
arm sleeve, clipboard) drawn LAST.

Basketball's universal silhouette tells that separate it from soccer: the
**sleeveless tank with bare shoulders + deep armholes**, **baggy long shorts**
(vs soccer's short shorts + tall socks), **chunky high-tops** (vs cleats), and
gear like **headbands, arm sleeves, wristbands**. Lean on those.

The five read as five different basketball people, so the SET can't be mistaken
for "one tank, five colours."

---

## DESIGN 1 — THE PRO (modern NBA, refined)
- **Hero read:** the clean modern pro uniform — a crisp **sleeveless tank with
  bare shoulders + deep armhole scoops**, a big bold white number, **baggy knee
  shorts** with a side stripe, a thin brow headband, and chunky **high-tops**.
  The bare-shoulder tank + long shorts + high-tops trio IS the hoops read.
- **Object list + placement:** sleeveless tank over torso (`BCY-6..+10`) with
  explicit armhole curves exposing scarlet shoulders + thin straps; big white
  number "23" on the chest; baggy shorts hem `BCY+10..+14` with a contrast side
  panel; thin headband at the brow; wristband on the near wing; white high-tops
  with an accent stripe + grey sole at the feet.
- **Palette:** `#E0632B` court orange · `#1A1C22` black trim · `#F4F4F8` white ·
  `#3B6BD6` accent · `#C8CCD4` sole grey.
- **Distinctness:** the textbook modern hooper — bare-shoulder tank + baggy
  shorts + high-tops, the clean baseline of the set.

## DESIGN 2 — THE STREETBALLER (and-1 / playground)
- **Hero read:** the playground legend — a **gold CHAIN necklace** (hero, drawn
  LAST over the chest), a **backwards snapback or do-rag** on the crown, a baggy
  reversible tank, an **arm SLEEVE** on the near wing, super-baggy shorts. Loud,
  streetwear, an era/attitude apart from the clean pro.
- **Object list + placement:** baggy tank over torso (looser, lower neckline);
  thick **gold rope chain** looping the neck + dipping to `BCX, BCY+1`;
  **backwards cap** (brim pointing back off the crown, `CROWN_Y`) OR a do-rag
  with a tail; **compression arm sleeve** down the near wing (`BCX+12, BCY..+12`);
  a sweatband; chunky high-tops with bold laces.
- **Palette:** `#16181E` blacktop black · `#E8B23A` gold chain · `#D8362F` red
  tank · `#F4F4F8` white · `#5A5E6B` sleeve grey.
- **Distinctness:** the CHAIN + backwards cap + arm sleeve = streetball, not a
  league uniform — the swagger design.

## DESIGN 3 — THE RETRO '90s (short-shorts era)
- **Hero read:** throwback hardwood — a **pinstriped tank**, classic **SHORT
  shorts** (not baggy), **tube socks pulled high** with twin stripe bands, and
  iconic **rec-specs / goggles** on the face. A clearly different *era* of
  basketball.
- **Object list + placement:** pinstriped tank over torso with a wide team
  wordmark band; SHORT shorts hem high at `BCY+8`; **high tube socks** with two
  hoop stripes on each leg (`HY+15..+23`) — a rare lower-leg tell for hoops;
  retro low sneakers; **goggles** across the eyes (clear lens + dark strap,
  must not cover the beak); flat-top headband.
- **Palette:** `#5B2E91` retro purple · `#E8B23A` gold pinstripe · `#F4F4F8`
  white · `#1A1C22` strap · `#2EA84F` accent.
- **Distinctness:** pinstripes + SHORT shorts + HIGH tube socks + goggles = '90s
  hardwood, the only design with a lower-leg sock tell.

## DESIGN 4 — THE ALL-STAR DUNKER (flashy showcase)
- **Hero read:** the dunk-contest showman — a **metallic GOLD/chrome uniform**
  with a star motif, a glossy **shooter ARM SLEEVE** full-length on the near
  wing (hero), a flashy headband, and gold-trim high-tops. Maximum bling, all
  spectacle.
- **Object list + placement:** shiny tank over torso with a big **STAR** behind
  the number + metallic trim piping; **full-length arm sleeve** down the near
  wing drawn LAST (`BCX+12, BCY-4..+13`) with a highlight seam; gold headband
  with a star; baggy shorts with star side-panels; gold-and-white high-tops
  with a glint.
- **Palette:** `#E8B23A` metallic gold · `#1A2150` midnight navy · `#F4F4F8`
  white star · `#B8862A` gold shadow · `#7CC9F0` chrome glint.
- **Distinctness:** the only METALLIC/star design + full arm sleeve = all-star
  spectacle, unmistakable from the matte pro tank.

## DESIGN 5 — THE COACH (sideline / clipboard)
- **Hero read:** the man on the bench — a sharp **blazer over a shirt + TIE**,
  a **whistle on a lanyard**, and a **CLIPBOARD held up in the near wing**
  (hero, drawn LAST). A totally different role + silhouette: tailored, not
  athletic — the basketball counterpart to the soccer referee.
- **Object list + placement:** open blazer over torso (lapels framing a shirt +
  knotted tie at the neck `BCY-9`); pocket square at the near breast; **whistle
  + lanyard** looping the neck to `BCX, BCY+2`; **clipboard** (small board +
  paper + a play diagram) held up in the near wing (`BCX+15, BCY-2`) breaking
  the silhouette; no athletic footwear emphasis (dress-shoe ticks at the feet).
- **Palette:** `#23304F` blazer navy · `#F4F4F8` white shirt · `#C2392B` tie
  red · `#1A1C22` lapel/shoe · `#E8B23A` whistle/lanyard gold.
- **Distinctness:** the only TAILORED silhouette — blazer + tie + clipboard =
  coach, the loudest break from every athletic kit and from the soccer set.
