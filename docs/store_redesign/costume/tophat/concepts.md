# TOP HAT — Gentleman Costume Concepts (`skin_tophat` / "TOP HAT")

Five distinct gentleman archetypes, each a FULL outfit layering head + face +
neck + body + limbs/feet across Pip the scarlet macaw. All keep a recognizable
**top hat** (the item's identity). Every concept is judged against a 40px-in-
motion truth read on day AND night skies: bold silhouette, strong value
contrast, one hero element, no fussy detail.

Canvas 64×100 · head centre HX=47 HY=41 · crown top CROWN_Y=31 (hat rises
above) · body centre ~(32,52) · feet ~(28,65)/(34,65) · wing/hand prop slung
diagonally from the body.

Numbers map to design_1 … design_5.

---

## 1. THE TYCOON — Gilded-Age Magnate (Monopoly-man)

**Why #1:** Most instantly legible gentleman read on the planet — a cartoon
shorthand everyone knows. Highest day/night contrast (white tie vs black),
strongest single hero prop (the dollar cane), and the broadest comedic charm.

- **Hero silhouette:** Towering black top hat over a fat white moustache and a
  bright white wing-collar shirt — a crisp white wedge floating under a black
  block.
- **Top hat:** Tall glossy black silk top hat, narrow satin band, slight curl
  to the brim. Rises well above CROWN_Y with a soft top highlight stripe.
- **Face:** Big bushy white walrus moustache splayed under the beak (the comedy
  tell at 40px); round gold-rimmed monocle on the near eye with a 1px glint.
- **Neck:** White wing-collar + slim black bow tie, sitting as a tight white
  triangle at the throat.
- **Body:** Black morning-coat painted OVER the scarlet body (keeps red as a
  thin lapel-edge accent), open to reveal a buff cream waistcoat with three
  dot buttons and a draped GOLD pocket-watch chain swag across the belly.
- **Limbs:** I-shaped black cane in the slung wing, capped with a glowing gold
  "$" disc at the grip — the legendary flourish.
- **Feet:** White spats over the feet with two side buttons.
- **Palette:** `#111114` (hat/coat), `#F4EFE2` (collar/shirt/spats),
  `#E8C24A` (gold chain/$ cane glow), `#D9C9A0` (waistcoat), `#7C0E12`
  (scarlet lapel edge).
- **Distinctness:** The only WHITE-dominant chest + literal money prop — wealth
  comedy, brightest value of the five.

---

## 2. THE RINGMASTER — Crimson Showman

**Why #2:** The boldest COLOR identity (crimson + gold), reads loudly on both
skies, and the gold frogging is a unique horizontal-stripe signature no other
concept has. Pure spectacle, naturally suits a legendary glint treatment.

- **Hero silhouette:** Black top hat above a crimson tailcoat blazing with
  three rows of gold horizontal frog-braid down the chest — a red torso ladder.
- **Top hat:** Black top hat with a CRIMSON satin band and a thin gold pinstripe
  edge on the brim.
- **Face:** Sharp upward-curled black handlebar moustache (the showman tell);
  no monocle — keep the face clean for the curls to pop.
- **Neck:** Tall black stand collar with a small gold-knot cravat.
- **Body:** Crimson tailcoat painted OVER the body (red stays!), with black
  cutaway lapels and THREE pairs of gold frogging bars + dot buttons stacked
  vertically; gold epaulette dots at the shoulders.
- **Limbs:** Slim black ringmaster cane (or coiled whip silhouette) in the
  slung wing, gold ferrule tip.
- **Feet:** Tall black riding boots with a gold cuff line.
- **Palette:** `#B0121A` (crimson coat), `#15151A` (hat/lapels/boots),
  `#E9C24C` (gold frogging — soft glow), `#F2D98A` (gold highlight),
  `#3A0508` (coat shadow).
- **Distinctness:** The only RED-coat / horizontal-gold-braid silhouette — loud
  circus energy where the others are sober formalwear.

## 3. THE LORD — Aristocratic Peer

**Why #3:** The richest, most ornate read — a diagonal sash + medals give a
unique angled accent line, and the deep-blue-and-gold palette is the most
"premium showpiece" of the formal set. Slightly busier, so ranked below the two
punchiest reads.

- **Hero silhouette:** Black top hat over a deep navy coat crossed by a bright
  gold-bordered SASH running shoulder-to-hip — a single bold diagonal stripe.
- **Top hat:** Black top hat, gold band, a tiny gold cockade pin on the side.
- **Face:** Neat grey pointed Vandyke goatee + thin moustache; gold-rimmed
  monocle on the near eye.
- **Neck:** White lace jabot/cravat spilling in a soft white blob at the throat.
- **Body:** Deep navy ceremonial coat painted OVER the body, with a RED-and-gold
  diagonal sash and 2–3 round medal discs clustered at the chest (gold/silver
  dots with tiny ribbon bars). Gold-trim coat edges.
- **Limbs:** Gold-topped ebony walking stick in the slung wing.
- **Feet:** Black court shoes with gold buckles.
- **Palette:** `#1B2A52` (navy coat), `#101018` (hat), `#E7C24A` (gold sash
  trim/medals — soft sheen), `#B41522` (sash field), `#EDE9DD` (jabot).
- **Distinctness:** The only DIAGONAL sash + medals + navy — ceremonial nobility,
  defined by an angled accent the four verticals lack.

---

## 4. THE UNDERTAKER — Gothic Nightmare Gentleman

**Why #4:** A striking all-dark counter to the bright four, with a skull-cane
hero prop and the most distinct MOOD. Ranked here only because all-black risks
disappearing on a night sky — it leans on a pale wing-collar + silver glints to
hold its read.

- **Hero silhouette:** Extra-tall black top hat over a long, narrow black frock
  coat — a slim dark tower broken by one pale collar slash and a silver skull
  glow.
- **Top hat:** Very tall matte-black top hat with a black crepe mourning band
  and a small wilted dark rose tucked in the band.
- **Face:** Gaunt pale-grey face accent; thin drooping black moustache; small
  round SMOKED monocle (dark lens, silver rim).
- **Neck:** Pale ash wing-collar + a black silk cravat (the one bright value
  that keeps the silhouette legible at night).
- **Body:** Long black frock coat painted OVER the body with charcoal-grey
  lapels (value separation from the band), a single jet button, and a thin
  silver watch-chain.
- **Limbs:** Black cane topped with a small glowing SILVER SKULL knob in the
  slung wing — the legendary flourish.
- **Feet:** Black buttoned ankle boots, dull silver spat buttons.
- **Palette:** `#0C0C10` (coat/hat), `#2A2A33` (charcoal lapels), `#C9C7CE`
  (collar/skull/silver — soft glow), `#6A6E78` (cool shadow), `#4A0E18`
  (wilted-rose accent).
- **Distinctness:** The only ALL-DARK, mournful palette with a skull prop —
  gothic gloom against four bright dandies.

## 5. THE DANDY — Jazz-Age Bon Vivant

**Why #5:** The most colorful-fun formal look (lavender + emerald) with a
boutonnière and spectacles, but its charm lives in mid-value pastels that fight
hardest for contrast at 40px — so it rounds out the set as the soft, stylish
option rather than the punchiest.

- **Hero silhouette:** Black top hat over a pale-lavender tailcoat with a vivid
  emerald waistcoat wedge and a single bright flower at the lapel.
- **Top hat:** Black top hat with a wide LAVENDER grosgrain band (the colorful
  twist on the hat itself).
- **Face:** Pencil-thin curled moustache; round tortoiseshell SPECTACLES (two
  small rings) instead of a monocle, for the jazz-age look.
- **Neck:** Bright emerald-and-gold ascot/cravat puffed at the throat.
- **Body:** Soft dove/lavender tailcoat painted OVER the body, open to a vivid
  emerald-green waistcoat with gold buttons and a slim gold watch-chain; a
  scarlet ROSE boutonnière at the lapel (a nod to Pip's native red).
- **Limbs:** Slim ivory-and-gold dress cane in the slung wing.
- **Feet:** Two-tone spectator spats (cream + tan).
- **Palette:** `#C8C2DA` (lavender coat), `#15171C` (hat), `#1F7A4D` (emerald
  waistcoat/ascot), `#E5BE48` (gold buttons/cane), `#C21B1E` (rose
  boutonnière).
- **Distinctness:** The only PASTEL + emerald, flower-and-spectacles look —
  playful dandy color where the others stay sober or loud-primary.

---

## Ranking summary

1. **THE TYCOON** — Gilded-age magnate (clearest read, money-cane flourish).
2. **THE RINGMASTER** — Crimson showman (boldest color, gold frogging).
3. **THE LORD** — Aristocratic peer (ornate sash + medals, premium navy/gold).
4. **THE UNDERTAKER** — Gothic nightmare gentleman (all-dark mood, skull cane).
5. **THE DANDY** — Jazz-age bon vivant (playful pastel + emerald + flower).

**Most legendary-worthy flourish:** the Tycoon's glowing gold "$" cane and the
Undertaker's silver skull knob both invite an animated glint/glow treatment.
