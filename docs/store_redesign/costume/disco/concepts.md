# DISCO Costume Concepts — Pip the Skybit Macaw

Five layered, physically-worn disco costumes for the 64×60 side-view parrot
sprite. Every concept dresses the bird in **multiple stacked objects** (head +
body + wing + feet/tail) so the read at 40px is "this parrot is WEARING a
disco outfit," not "this parrot is tinted." Procedural-only: gradients,
polygons, glows, tiny sparkle particles — no raster textures.

Numbers map to builders: concept **1 → design_1**, … **5 → design_5**.

Archetype spread (deliberately non-overlapping):
1. **BOOGIE NIGHTS** — Saturday Night Fever floor-filler (early leisure-suit glam)
2. **STARDUST DIVA** — Studio 54 peak-glam star (sequin + boa spectacle)
3. **ROLLER GROOVE** — late-70s roller-rink kid (athletic, primary-color fun)
4. **THE SELECTOR** — funky-soul booth DJ (headphones + vinyl, cool status)
5. **MIRRORBALL** — the walking disco ball (legendary showpiece / the flex)

---

## 1. BOOGIE NIGHTS

- **Hero silhouette:** A parrot ballooned into a wide-shouldered white suit,
  crowned by a fat round afro — a lollipop-headed dancer frozen mid-strut.
- **Costume objects:**
  - **HEAD:** oversized round **afro** (deep-brown, matte, chunky outline) that
    doubles the head's diameter and reads as a bold dark disc; thin gold pick
    tucked in the side.
  - **BODY:** open-chest **white three-piece suit** — huge pointed lapels
    framing an exposed chest, a big **gold medallion** hanging center-chest as
    the high-contrast tell.
  - **WING/ARM:** flared **bell-bottom cuff** buttoned at the wing base so each
    flap flares the sleeve open like a bell.
  - **FEET/TAIL:** stacked **platform boots** in cream with a chunky wooden
    heel; tail pokes out below the jacket hem.
- **Palette:** `#F4EFE6` suit-white · `#E8B923` medallion-gold · `#5A3A21`
  afro-brown · `#C24A2E` shirt-rust · `#2B2622` deep-shadow line.
- **Distinctness:** The only earthy, matte, "suited-gentleman" look — warm
  brown + cream, zero sequins, all silhouette (afro + lapels) rather than shine.

---

## 2. STARDUST DIVA

- **Hero silhouette:** A slinky parrot drowning in a fluffy feather boa with a
  tall plumed headdress — a top-heavy, glittering showgirl outline.
- **Costume objects:**
  - **HEAD:** jeweled **glitter headband** with a tall single **ostrich-feather
    plume** curving up and back — the vertical spike that IDs it instantly.
  - **BODY:** sequined **halter jumpsuit** with a deep V-neckline, drawn as rows
    of tiny bright facets that catch a moving highlight (animated shimmer band).
  - **WING/ARM:** a **feather boa** looped around the neck and trailing over the
    near wing — soft scalloped fluff that flutters on the flap.
  - **FEET/TAIL:** strappy **silver platform heels**; tail styled into a
    sequined fan that reads as a glamorous train.
- **Palette:** `#E5237E` fuchsia · `#7A2AB0` grape · `#F2C63D` gold-sparkle ·
  `#D9DCE6` silver-facet · glow note: cycling white→cyan sequin glint + 8 star
  sparkles.
- **Distinctness:** The pure-glam vertical showpiece — hot fuchsia/purple, soft
  boa fluff, and a plume that makes it the tallest, most "diva" silhouette.

---

## 3. ROLLER GROOVE

- **Hero silhouette:** A sporty parrot on fat rainbow-wheeled skates with a
  sweatband and striped tank — low, wheeled, and bouncy.
- **Costume objects:**
  - **HEAD:** terrycloth **sweatband** across the brow with a triple stripe;
    a small feathered flick of "hair" over the top.
  - **BODY:** striped **ribbed tank top** (rainbow chevron band across the
    chest) with a zip-pull; short satin **hot-pants** at the hip.
  - **WING/ARM:** matching terry **wristbands** at each wing base; a lightning
    or star iron-on decal on the wing.
  - **FEET/TAIL:** classic **quad roller skates** — white boots, red toe-stops,
    and chunky multicolor wheels with a tiny motion-blur streak (the tell).
- **Palette:** `#2FB6C4` teal · `#F25C2A` orange · `#F4D03F` yellow ·
  `#FFFFFF` boot-white · `#D6335A` toe-stop-red.
- **Distinctness:** The athletic, primary-color, ground-gear look — the only
  one defined by footwear (wheels) and sportswear rather than eveningwear.

---

## 4. THE SELECTOR

- **Hero silhouette:** A cool parrot behind chunky foam headphones holding a
  vinyl record like a shield — boxy, gadget-headed, laid-back.
- **Costume objects:**
  - **HEAD:** large foam **DJ headphones** with a thick band arcing over the
    skull and one big cup over the ear; tinted **aviator shades** on the face.
  - **BODY:** wide-collar **satin shirt open over a turtleneck** with a slim
    corduroy blazer — earthy, buttoned-cool, no skin-shine.
  - **WING/ARM:** the near wing holds a spinning **vinyl record** (black disc,
    colored label, glossy sheen sweep) — the standout prop.
  - **FEET/TAIL:** platform **suede clogs**; tail flat and relaxed.
- **Palette:** `#B5561E` burnt-orange · `#6B4A2B` chestnut · `#2A2A2E` vinyl-
  black · `#C9CDD2` chrome-headphone · `#E8A03C` record-label.
- **Distinctness:** The only "status/craft" figure — muted 70s earth tones,
  tech (headphones) + prop (record), reads as the person running the party, not
  dancing on the floor.

---

## 5. MIRRORBALL  *(legendary showpiece)*

- **Hero silhouette:** A parrot fused into a faceted mirror ball — a glittering
  sphere on wings shooting little beams of light in every direction.
- **Costume objects:**
  - **HEAD:** a **mirror-ball helmet** of tiny reflective diamond tiles wrapping
    the head, each facet a shifting cyan/magenta/white glint.
  - **BODY:** a **mirror-tile vest** continuing the faceted grid down the chest;
    beak and eyes peek through so it still reads as Pip.
  - **WING/ARM:** wing edges lined with **light-beam rays** — thin animated
    spokes that sweep as it flaps, like a spotlight hitting the ball.
  - **FEET/TAIL:** a small **chrome pedestal / spotlight cone** under the feet;
    tail trails a sparkle stream of drifting light dots.
- **Palette:** `#DfE6F0` mirror-silver · `#3FE0FF` cyan-glint · `#FF3FB8`
  magenta-glint · `#FFF6C9` hot-white-core · glow note: full-time rotating
  facet shimmer + radial beam sweep + 8–12 twinkling light particles (this is
  the baked-in spectacle that justifies the legendary tier).
- **Distinctness:** The only object that *replaces* the bird's surface with an
  environment prop (the disco ball itself) and the only full-time animated-glow
  flex — the showpiece the other four aspire to dance under.

---

## Ranking

1. **MIRRORBALL** — strongest concept and the clear legendary showpiece; the
   "become the disco ball" gag is instantly readable and the animated facet +
   beam shimmer is a genuine flex.
2. **BOOGIE NIGHTS** — the most iconic, universally-legible disco read (afro +
   white suit + medallion); anchors the roster in real Saturday-Night-Fever DNA.
3. **STARDUST DIVA** — the best pure-glam pick; tall plume + boa give it a
   unique tall silhouette no other concept touches.
4. **ROLLER GROOVE** — the fun, energetic outlier; wheels + primary colors keep
   the set from being all eveningwear.
5. **THE SELECTOR** — coolest/most niche; headphones + vinyl are great but the
   earth-tone palette is the least "sparkly," so it ranks last for pure disco pop.

**Best legendary showpiece:** MIRRORBALL (concept 5).
