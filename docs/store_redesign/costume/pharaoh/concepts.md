# PHARAOH — full Egyptian outfit concepts (`skin_pharaoh`, 700-coin premium)

Brief: enrich the head-only nemes-on-scarlet costume into a FULL themed Egyptian
outfit. 5 distinct ancient-Egyptian royalty/mythology archetypes — each a full
layered outfit (head + face + neck/chest + body + limbs/feet), each a clean
**40px-in-motion** read on BOTH day and night skies, each lavish/legendary
enough to justify 700 coins.

Footprint law (HARD): hitbox is a fixed ~10px circle at body centre and never
changes. Keep ALL body-worn elements (collar, skirt, recolor, slung props)
WITHIN the base bird footprint — body centre ~(32,52), feet line y65-69. NOTHING
hangs below the feet line; NOTHING balloons the body. ONLY headgear may rise
above CROWN_Y=31. Slung wing/hand props read diagonally across the body, tucked
inside the silhouette, not dangling past feet.

Coord anchors: canvas 64×100; head centre HX=47, HY=41; crown top CROWN_Y=31;
body centre ~(32,52); feet ~(28,65)/(34,65).

Research grounding: nemes = lapis-blue + gold stripes with uraeus cobra at brow;
gold/yellow = the eternal flesh of the gods; Anubis = black jackal (black =
death + Nile-soil fertility); Ra = falcon head, sun-disk encircled by a cobra,
golden skin; crook & flail = Osiris/kingship; Cleopatra/queen = vulture or modius
crown, kohl eyes, beaded usekh collar.

---

## 1. GOLDEN PHARAOH — Tutankhamun, the boy-king (the classic)

- **Archetype:** the iconic golden funerary-mask pharaoh.
- **Hero silhouette:** the wide flaring **nemes headdress** — trapezoid wings
  sweeping down past the cheeks — capped by a fat gold brow-band.
- **Object list + placement:**
  - *Headgear (above CROWN_Y):* striped nemes, lapis-blue + gold horizontal
    stripes, flaring shoulder lappets framing the head; gold uraeus cobra
    rearing at the brow.
  - *Face:* short braided **false beard** straight down from the chin (within
    head footprint); thin kohl line.
  - *Neck/chest:* broad **usekh collar**, concentric gold + turquoise bead rows
    arcing across the upper breast — sits inside body footprint.
  - *Body:* **gold body recolor** (eternal-flesh gold) so the whole bird glows.
  - *Limbs:* miniature **crook & flail** crossed and slung diagonally in the
    wing, tucked inside the silhouette.
  - *Feet:* gold anklets at the feet line.
- **Palette:** `#F4C430` gold, `#1B3A8C` lapis blue, `#2FB8A6` turquoise,
  `#7A4A12` deep bronze shadow, glint `#FFF2B0`. Day: gold pops on blue sky;
  night: lapis stripes + bronze shadow keep value structure; gold glint glow.
- **Body treatment:** **body recolor via base palette** (gold).
- **Distinctness:** the only all-gold, nemes-and-collar *king* — the textbook
  pharaoh silhouette, max bling, crook & flail prop.

## 2. ANUBIS — the jackal god of the dead

- **Archetype:** Anubis, black-jackal embalmer deity.
- **Hero silhouette:** two tall pointed **jackal ears** spiking straight up
  above the head — unmistakable at 40px, totally unlike any nemes.
- **Object list + placement:**
  - *Headgear (above CROWN_Y):* tall erect black jackal ears; thin gold
    inner-ear lining for contrast on night sky.
  - *Face:* gold-rimmed almond eyes + long curved gold kohl flick (jackal
    muzzle implied by a dark snout shadow on the beak).
  - *Neck/chest:* narrow gold-and-black striped collar band.
  - *Body:* **jackal-black body recolor** with a faint blue-black sheen.
  - *Limbs:* gold **was-scepter** (forked-base staff) slung diagonally in the
    wing; small white **ankh** glyph at the hip.
  - *Feet:* gold anklets.
- **Palette:** `#0E0E14` jackal black, `#1A2238` blue-black sheen, `#E8B53A`
  gold, `#F5F0E6` ankh ivory. Day: black bird reads as a hard silhouette vs
  bright sky; night: gold ears-lining + ankh + collar carry it.
- **Body treatment:** **body recolor via base palette** (black).
- **Distinctness:** the only god-beast — pointed-ear silhouette + black body +
  was-scepter; dark where the others are gold.

## 3. THE MUMMY — the bandaged risen king

- **Archetype:** wrapped royal mummy, eerie and funny.
- **Hero silhouette:** the **linen-wrapped bird** — body banded in horizontal
  bandage strips with one loose end curling off the back (inside footprint).
- **Object list + placement:**
  - *Headgear (above CROWN_Y):* head wrapped in linen with a slim gold
    brow-band; one bandage tail flicking up.
  - *Face:* two **sunken hollow eyes** — dark voids with a faint inner pinpoint
    glow; gap in the wrap shows the eyes.
  - *Neck/chest:* single large **scarab amulet** (deep-blue glazed beetle) on
    the chest — the lone splash of colour.
  - *Body:* **linen-white body recolor** painted as overlapping bandage bands
    (aged cream, grey seams) — kept strictly within footprint.
  - *Limbs:* arms crossed-over-chest mummy pose hinted in the wing wrap; a short
    loose bandage end slung in the wing (does NOT cross feet line).
  - *Feet:* wrapped feet, frayed bandage tips at the feet line (not below).
- **Palette:** `#EDE4CF` aged linen, `#C8B690` shadow seam, `#8A7A55` grime,
  `#1E5FB0` scarab blue, `#E8B53A` gold band. Day: cream body + blue scarab pop;
  night: gold band + glowing scarab + eye-pinpoints keep it alive.
- **Body treatment:** **body recolor via base palette** (linen-white) painted as
  bandage bands.
- **Distinctness:** the only pale/textured (wrapped) body, hollow-eye horror tell
  and scarab amulet — value-inverted from gold and black concepts.

## 4. CLEOPATRA — the queen

- **Archetype:** royal queen / consort (Cleopatra-Hathor).
- **Hero silhouette:** the **vulture crown** — a domed cap hugging the head
  topped by a small modius disk, distinct rounded crown unlike nemes or ears.
- **Object list + placement:**
  - *Headgear (above CROWN_Y):* gold vulture/modius crown — rounded skullcap with
    a slim sun-disk between two upright plumes; small uraeus at front.
  - *Face:* dramatic **Cleopatra kohl eyes** — extended black liner with a long
    upturned tail and a teal lid accent.
  - *Neck/chest:* wide **beaded Cleopatra collar** — rows of teal, carnelian-red
    and gold beads (richer/cooler than the gold pharaoh's), within footprint.
  - *Body:* **paint over scarlet** — keep a warm linen-white painted sheath dress
    with thin gold straps, leaving hints of the macaw red as trim.
  - *Limbs:* golden **lotus-flower scepter** slung in the wing.
  - *Feet:* gold anklets + sandal straps.
- **Palette:** `#E9C46A` gold, `#2A9D8F` teal, `#C1453B` carnelian, `#1C1C22`
  kohl black, `#F2E8D5` linen sheath. Day: teal+carnelian collar pops; night:
  gold crown + kohl eyes + teal beads hold contrast.
- **Body treatment:** **paint over scarlet body** (linen sheath dress, red trim).
- **Distinctness:** the only feminine/regal read — rounded crown, signature
  swept kohl eyes, cool teal-carnelian palette (vs warm gold), lotus scepter.

## 5. RA — the sun-falcon (LEGENDARY showpiece)

- **Archetype:** Ra, sun-god, solar-disk falcon — the flex.
- **Hero silhouette:** a huge glowing **sun disk** crowning the head, encircled
  by a rearing cobra — a radiant halo silhouette that reads instantly and glows.
- **Object list + placement:**
  - *Headgear (above CROWN_Y):* large gold **solar disk** ringed by a coiled gold
    uraeus, with a soft animated glow/aura (the legendary glint treatment).
  - *Face:* falcon-style eye markings — a sharp gold-and-black Eye-of-Ra liner
    sweeping back from the eye.
  - *Neck/chest:* **winged sun collar** — usekh collar whose outer rows fan into
    stylised falcon-wing feathers in gold + sky-blue + red bands.
  - *Body:* **gold body recolor** with subtle warm-to-amber gradient (solar
    sheen) — within footprint.
  - *Limbs:* gold **was-scepter** + small ankh slung in the wing.
  - *Feet:* gold talon-anklets at the feet line.
- **Palette:** `#FFD24A` sun gold, `#FF8A1E` solar amber, `#C0331F` falcon red,
  `#2E6FB0` sky blue, glow `#FFF6C8`. Day: amber disk + glow reads as a tiny sun;
  night: the disk's bloom makes it the brightest sprite on screen.
- **Body treatment:** **body recolor via base palette** (gold/amber gradient).
- **Distinctness:** the only one with an animated radiant glow + halo silhouette
  and winged collar — the legendary spectacle pick justifying the 700-coin tier.

## Ranking (best first → maps to design_1…design_5)

1. **GOLDEN PHARAOH (Tutankhamun)** — the must-have canonical pharaoh; instantly
   reads, max bling, satisfies "keep one classic nemes-and-collar."
2. **ANUBIS** — strongest *distinct* silhouette (pointed ears, black body); the
   boldest departure and a fan-favourite god.
3. **RA (sun-falcon)** — best legendary showpiece; glow halo is the flex, but
   ranked under the two cleaner everyday reads.
4. **THE MUMMY** — great novelty + value-inverted pale body; slightly fussier
   wrap detail to keep clean at 40px.
5. **CLEOPATRA** — lovely and distinct, but the rounded crown + paint-over body
   is the subtlest silhouette of the five at 40px.

**Strongest overall pick:** GOLDEN PHARAOH. **Best legendary showpiece:** RA.
