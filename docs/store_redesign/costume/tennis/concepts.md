# TENNIS costume — higher-fidelity exploration (5 directions)

**Base = the current shipped tennis** (`tools/sports_candidates/design_5.py` /
live `skin_tennis`): white polo on the chest, ONE green oval strung racket held
up in the near wing (head breaks the silhouette like the pirate cutlass), brow
visor, green sash + wristbands. No ball (ships as a separate parcel).

These 5 take that base and push **fidelity** — every version MUST:

1. **RACKET** — ONE clear, well-crafted racket: a crisp single oval frame (no
   "circle-inside-a-circle"), clean readable strings (mains + crosses, not mush),
   a proper **throat Y** + **wrapped handle/grip + butt cap**, and 3-value frame
   shading so the oval reads round at 40px. Drawn LAST so the whole prop sits in
   FRONT of the body; the head still breaks the top/back silhouette.
2. **POLO/SHIRT** — a better-shaped polo on the **chest BELOW the head** (top
   ~HY+8, hem ~HY+23, like the baseball jersey), never rising into / covering
   Pip's beak/eye/face. Rounded cloth shading (lit upper + cool lower shade +
   dark contour so the white/colour stays crisp), a real collar V, a placket, and
   a sport accent (stripe/sash/piping). Held INSIDE the footprint — no ballooning.
3. **Crispness** — max detail within the fixed 64×100 canvas; legible day AND
   night at the 40px truth read.

Anchors (composite space): `HX=47, HY=41, CROWN_Y=31`; body centre `(32,52)`;
feet `(28,65)/(34,65)`. Racket head held around `(HX-21, CROWN_Y+2)`.

Each design is distinct in **palette + headgear + racket style** so the five read
as five different tennis pros, not five recolors.

---

## DESIGN 1 — WIMBLEDON WHITES (the classic)
- **Hero read:** crisp all-white grass-court tradition; royal-green + aubergine
  (Wimbledon purple) trim. The most "pure tennis" of the set.
- **Headgear:** a thin terry **headband** at the brow (white with a green+purple
  twin stripe), crown left open so Pip clearly reads as the macaw.
- **Racket:** classic **wood-frame** look — warm cream/ivory frame (not green),
  thin green throat struts, tan leather grip with cross-wrap ticks, butt cap.
  Tight clean string bed (pale strings on a dark void).
- **Polo:** bright white, green collar V + green-purple twin placket stripe, a
  small chest crest; cool lower shade rounds the cloth.
- **Palette:** `#F6F6F2` white · `#1E7A3C` royal green · `#4B2E66` aubergine ·
  `#E7D9A8` cream wood frame · `#7A5A33` leather grip.
- **Distinctness:** ivory WOOD racket + headband + purple/green trim = heritage.

## DESIGN 2 — CLAY COURT (Roland-Garros terracotta)
- **Hero read:** warm clay-court palette — terracotta/ochre accents on white,
  the dusty orange of a European clay season. Sun-drenched, earthy.
- **Headgear:** a white **visor** (like the base) but cleaner — curved brim,
  terracotta band trim across the brow.
- **Racket:** modern **graphite** frame in deep clay-orange (`#C9622E`) with a
  cream throat bridge; orange frame, white strings, dark grip. One bold oval.
- **Polo:** white with a terracotta side panel + orange piping down the placket;
  ochre wristband on the near wing.
- **Palette:** `#F4F1EA` warm white · `#C9622E` terracotta · `#E08A3C` ochre ·
  `#8A3E1C` clay shadow · `#2C2622` dark grip.
- **Distinctness:** the only WARM-tone kit; clay-orange racket + visor.

## DESIGN 3 — NEON BASELINER (modern athletic)
- **Hero read:** bright modern sportswear — electric blue + volt/lime, the look
  of a current hard-court night session under lights. High-energy, saturated.
- **Headgear:** a sleek **volt headband** (electric-blue band, lime edge),
  crown open.
- **Racket:** a **tech graphite** frame in electric blue with a lime throat +
  bumper; volt-green strings on a near-black void so the bed glows; matte grip.
- **Polo:** athletic-cut in cobalt + lime, a diagonal volt slash across the
  chest, dark mesh side panel for shape; volt wristband.
- **Palette:** `#1450C8` electric blue · `#B6F23A` volt/lime · `#0C2E78` deep
  cobalt · `#F2F4F0` white trim · `#15171C` matte grip.
- **Distinctness:** the COLD high-saturation kit; glowing lime string bed — best
  night read of the set.

## DESIGN 4 — RETRO '70s (vintage cream)
- **Hero read:** vintage tennis — cream/ecru with a red + navy retro stripe
  band, old-school wood racket. Warm nostalgia, throwback club look.
- **Headgear:** a thick **terry sweatband** (cream with a red/navy/red triple
  stripe) — the iconic '70s tennis brow band.
- **Racket:** classic **wood** racket, honey-tan frame with a darker laminate
  edge, a tall narrow oval head, leather grip + visible string-saver weave;
  small painted throat decal.
- **Polo:** ecru/cream with retro **shawl collar**, red+navy chest piping, navy
  short sleeves trim; matching wristband.
- **Palette:** `#EFE6CE` ecru · `#C2392B` retro red · `#1E2A55` navy ·
  `#B5853F` honey wood · `#6E4A22` dark laminate.
- **Distinctness:** vintage cream + tri-stripe sweatband + honey wood racket.

## DESIGN 5 — NIGHT MATCH (hard-court blue)
- **Hero read:** deep hard-court blue (US/Australian-Open style) — navy + cyan
  on white, built to POP on the night sky. The "stadium under lights" version.
- **Headgear:** a white **cap with a curved brim** (like a tennis cap) in white
  with a navy panel + cyan button — gives a different silhouette from the
  visor/headband designs.
- **Racket:** a **graphite** frame in navy with a cyan inner bumper line, white
  strings on a dark void, a clean throat Y, dark grip + cyan butt cap.
- **Polo:** white with a navy yoke across the shoulders + cyan sash on the chest,
  navy collar; cyan wristband.
- **Palette:** `#F4F6F8` white · `#16306B` navy · `#2BB8E0` cyan ·
  `#0C1E4A` deep navy · `#20242C` grip.
- **Distinctness:** the only CAP (curved brim) silhouette; navy+cyan stadium kit.
