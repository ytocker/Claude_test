# Astronaut costume — v2 sibling concepts

Three NEW concepts that live in the same **white / realistic-modern space-suit
lane** as the two picked winners (MOONWALKER, STARLINER), but each clearly
distinct from them and from each other. Each layers helmet + suited torso +
a back element + chest/limb details so the silhouette breaks all over.

Ideation only — no art, no code. Concepts 1–3 map to `design_6`, `design_7`,
`design_8`.

Winners we riff on (do NOT duplicate):
- **MOONWALKER** — NASA-white EVA, bubble helmet + GOLD visor DOWN, chunky white
  PLSS brick, blue arm stripe, moon boots.
- **STARLINER** — SpaceX-sleek near-white, small ANGULAR BLACK visor + cyan chest
  accent, slim black flight-pack, black yoke/gloves/boots.

Axis coverage so the trio reads as distinct at 40px:

| # | Concept | Visor | Accent | Back element | Silhouette weight | Face |
|---|---------|-------|--------|--------------|-------------------|------|
| 1 | ARTEMIS | clear bubble (face shows) | NASA blue + red stripes | hard PLSS brick | puffy hard-torso | UP / clear |
| 2 | TRAILBLAZER | gold reflective DOWN | amber + dusty tan | big surface pack + antenna | rugged, tool-laden | DOWN |
| 3 | NOVA DRIFTER | chrome MIRROR DOWN | navy + safety-orange | RCS jetpack, flared nozzles | sleek + winged jets | DOWN |

## 1. ARTEMIS  →  `design_6`   (TOP PICK)

*Inspired by the real Axiom/Prada AxEMU Artemis III moon suit — white with the
iconic blue + red identification trim and a clear visor that shows the face.*

- **Tier-feel:** flagship "real next-gen NASA" sibling — the one that most
  obviously belongs next to MOONWALKER while looking newer and friendlier.
- **Hero silhouette:** a PUFFY hard-upper-torso astronaut — broad rounded
  shoulders and a fat bubble helmet, the bulkiest of the three, with a clean
  CLEAR dome so Pip's macaw face beams through. The blue+red shoulder/leg
  candy-stripes are the instant tell against the white.
- **Visor: UP / CLEAR.** Round transparent bubble dome with a soft top
  highlight; macaw eye + beak fully visible inside (warm, approachable — the
  opposite of MOONWALKER's hidden gold face). A thin chrome helmet ring frames
  the dome.
- **Layered objects:**
  - *Head:* clear bubble helmet + chrome neck ring; tiny side comms nub.
  - *Torso:* white hard-upper-torso shell (slightly inflated, rounded), a
    rectangular chest control panel with 2–3 cyan/green status pips, and a small
    round mission patch on one shoulder.
  - *Back:* hard white PLSS brick squared off past the crown — boxier/cleaner
    than MOONWALKER's, with a top vent slot.
  - *Limbs:* the signature Artemis identity stripes — a BLUE band + a RED band
    wrapping each upper arm and the leg/boot; white gloves; white boots with a
    blue sole line.
- **Palette:** `#F3F5FA` suit-white · `#1C3FA8` Artemis blue · `#D32030` Artemis
  red · `#2A2D34` keyline/visor-ring shadow · `#27D0B0` chest status pip.
- **Distinct because:** only concept with a CLEAR visor (face shown) AND the
  twin blue+red stripe identity — reads as "the official moon suit." Bulkiest
  hard-torso; MOONWALKER hides the face behind gold, STARLINER is sleek-black,
  TRAILBLAZER/NOVA are gold/mirror down.

## 2. TRAILBLAZER  →  `design_7`

*Inspired by the Apollo A7L lunar-surface EVA — gold LEVA visor down, big surface
PLSS, an antenna and a chest-mounted tool/utility harness, dust-stained lower
legs.*

- **Tier-feel:** rugged "been-on-the-surface" sibling. Reuses the gold-visor
  idea from MOONWALKER but pushes it into a tool-laden, weathered explorer with
  its own outline.
- **Hero silhouette:** a chunky surface walker bristling with gear — a single
  thin ANTENNA whip rising off the backpack is the unique top tell, plus a
  gold mirror dome and a tool-festooned chest. Lower legs read DUSTY (tan),
  grounding it as a planet-surface suit, not an orbital one.
- **Visor: DOWN / GOLD reflective.** Full gold convex visor, one soft diagonal
  glint; face hidden behind warm mirror gold (warmer + more orange-amber than
  MOONWALKER's flatter gold to read as a sibling, not a clone).
- **Layered objects:**
  - *Head:* gold visor dome + white helmet shell; a short **antenna whip** with
    a tiny tip-bead curving up off the pack (breaks the crown line — its
    signature).
  - *Torso:* white suit with a chest **utility harness** — a couple of dark
    strap lines crossing the chest holding 2 small tool/pouch nubs and an
    amber RCU display block.
  - *Back:* OVERSIZED white surface PLSS pack, taller and rounder than
    MOONWALKER's brick, with a side hose loop curving to the chest.
  - *Limbs:* white upper arms/gloves; lower legs + boots shaded DUSTY TAN
    (regolith staining) with a darker scuff line — unique grounding cue.
- **Palette:** `#EFF1F6` suit-white · `#E79A1F` warm gold visor · `#C9A56B`
  lunar-dust tan · `#3A2E22` strap/keyline brown-black · `#F2C14E` amber
  display.
- **Distinct because:** the ANTENNA + chest tool-harness + DUSTY tan legs make a
  busy, earthy surface-explorer outline no other concept has. Its gold is
  warm-amber vs MOONWALKER's cooler gold, and unlike Artemis (clear) and Nova
  (mirror) it owns the "field-worn" niche.

## 3. NOVA DRIFTER  →  `design_8`

*Inspired by NASA's MMU / SAFER maneuvering jetpacks (24 GN2 nozzles, side hand
controllers) + the EMU two-way mirror visor and commander striping.*

- **Tier-feel:** sleek, high-energy "free-flyer" sibling — the showpiece of the
  three, with a jetpack whose flared nozzles become its silhouette.
- **Hero silhouette:** SLEEKEST build (close to STARLINER's weight) but with a
  winged JETPACK — squared maneuvering unit whose little thruster nozzles flare
  out past the back/sides like fins, plus a flat CHROME mirror visor. The
  jet-wings are the unmistakable tell; the flap can puff faint thruster glints
  at the nozzles.
- **Visor: DOWN / MIRROR (chrome-silver).** Flat silver mirror dome with a cool
  blue-white sheen and one crisp horizontal glint line — colder and more
  reflective than MOONWALKER's gold or Nova's would-be amber; reads "polished."
- **Layered objects:**
  - *Head:* chrome mirror helmet dome + navy helmet collar; a single
    safety-orange comms cap stripe over the crown.
  - *Torso:* white suit with a NAVY shoulder yoke and one RED commander stripe
    down the arm; compact chest controller with an orange toggle and blue pips.
  - *Back:* squared white **RCS jetpack** with 3–4 short thruster NOZZLES poking
    out at the corners/sides (the outline-breaker), faint jet glow optional on
    flap; two small side hand-controller arms hint at the edges.
  - *Limbs:* navy gloves + boots; thin orange cuff rings.
- **Palette:** `#F4F6FB` suit-white · `#16223F` command navy · `#FF7A1A`
  safety-orange · `#B9C4D6` chrome mirror visor · `#D32030` commander red.
- **Distinct because:** the only one with a propulsive jetpack — flared nozzles
  give it a winged silhouette nothing else has, plus a CHROME mirror visor and a
  navy+orange "command" palette. Sleek like STARLINER but unmistakably different
  via the jet-wings and color story; vs Artemis (puffy/blue-red/clear) and
  Trailblazer (gold/dusty/antenna) it's the cold, fast orbital one.

---

## Ranking

1. **ARTEMIS (`design_6`)** — strongest, most current real-world hook (the
   actual Artemis III suit), clearest face via the clear visor, and the
   blue+red stripes are an iconic 40px tell. The natural "next to MOONWALKER"
   pick.
2. **NOVA DRIFTER (`design_8`)** — best spectacle and the most distinct
   silhouette (flared jetpack nozzles + chrome mirror visor); navy+orange gives
   the set a third clean color story.
3. **TRAILBLAZER (`design_7`)** — great rugged contrast and the antenna/dusty
   legs are charming, but it leans nearest to MOONWALKER (gold visor + big white
   pack), so it ranks third; the antenna, tool harness, and tan legs are what
   keep it clearly separate.

**Best showpiece:** NOVA DRIFTER — the jetpack nozzles + mirror visor + optional
thruster glints on flap are the flex.
