# SKIN: SOCCER — "Pip the Pro Footballer" — 5 Costume Concepts

Full-body re-skins that dress the macaw silhouette (head, back, body, wings,
tail, beak, feet) in a professional soccer kit. **Hard constraint honoured:**
no ball — Pip *wears* the kit, Pip *is* the design. Every concept layers
multiple themed objects across at least four body zones so the bird never
reads as "a parrot holding one accessory."

Numbers map to `design_1` … `design_5` for the parallel design loops. Ranking
(buildability + impact at ~40px) is given at the bottom; concept order below is
the ranked order (1 = strongest).

Research anchors: Brazil canary-yellow + green with thick collar; Netherlands
1974 all-orange with black shoulder stripes; Argentina sky-blue/white vertical
stripes; classic green long-sleeve keeper jersey + cap heritage + bright
high-vis keeper colourways; Barcelona blaugrana stripes; FIFA Law 4 kit
(jersey, shorts, socks-over-shinpads, studded boots) + captain's armband on the
left sleeve.

---

## 1. THE STRIKER — `skin_soccer` (design_1)

- **Silhouette:** A bold canary-yellow torso with a fat dark-green collar ring,
  a giant high-contrast **number 10** filling the chest/back, and bright white
  boots with a black stud row on the feet. The yellow body + green collar + big
  numeral is the single loudest soccer read on the roster — pure Brazil striker.
- **Object list + placement:**
  - HEAD: short black hair tuft over the crown; beak unchanged (natural).
  - CHEST/BODY: canary-yellow jersey block; thick dark-green crew collar as a
    ring at the neck; oversized white "10" centred on the chest.
  - BACK: same white "10" mirrored large on the back jersey.
  - WINGS: yellow sleeves with a single green cuff band at each wingtip.
  - FEET: white cleats, black sole + 3-stud row; green socks with a white
    horizontal pad band (shin guard hint).
  - TAIL: green shorts waistband band where tail meets body; tail tip yellow.
- **Palette:** `#F5D000` jersey primary · `#0A7A3C` collar/socks/shorts ·
  `#FFFFFF` boots + number · `#1A1A1A` hair/stud row · `#0B3A1E` outline.
- **Distinctness:** The only number-forward "goal-scorer" look; sunshine
  yellow/green pops hardest at 40px and against night sky. Unlike any existing
  skin (no metal, robes, hats, or bones) and is the cleanest single-team read.

---

## 2. THE GOALKEEPER — `skin_keeper` (design_2)

- **Silhouette:** The odd-one-out kit — a high-vis **lime-green** body, a soft
  peaked **keeper cap** on the head, and chunky padded **gloves at both
  wingtips** that read as oversized mitts when the wings flap. Cap + glove-tips
  is a silhouette no other concept (or skin) has.
- **Object list + placement:**
  - HEAD: classic peaked goalkeeper cap (lime crown + black brim) tilted on the
    crown.
  - CHEST/BODY: lime-green long-sleeve jersey with a thin black chevron yoke
    across the shoulders (1990s keeper look); small "1" on the chest.
  - BACK: large black "1" on the back.
  - WINGS: padded gloves at each wingtip — rounded black-and-yellow latex pads
    with a wide wrist strap; sleeves lime with black cuffs.
  - FEET: black cleats; lime socks with black stripe; shin-guard bulge.
  - TAIL: black shorts waistband; tail dipped lime.
- **Palette:** `#C7F000` jersey/cap primary · `#111111` brim/gloves/number ·
  `#FFC400` glove accent · `#3A8A00` shadow/socks · `#0E2400` outline.
- **Distinctness:** Position-driven, not nationality-driven — the only keeper,
  the only cap, the only glove-tipped wings. Acid lime is unused by every other
  skin and screams "different player." The flap animation gets a unique payoff:
  big catching mitts opening and closing.

---

## 3. EL CAPITÁN — `skin_captain` (design_3)

- **Silhouette:** Argentina-style **sky-blue + white vertical stripes** down the
  whole body, with a bright **captain's armband** wrapped high on one wing like
  a bicep band, and a sweat **headband** across the crown. Stripes + armband =
  leadership read.
- **Object list + placement:**
  - HEAD: white headband across the forehead/crown; small hair tuft above.
  - CHEST/BODY: vertical sky-blue and white stripes; thin navy V-neck collar.
  - BACK: navy "5" (classic captain/midfield number) between the stripes.
  - WINGS: one upper wing wears the **captain's armband** (gold band with a
    navy "C"); both sleeves carry alternating stripe cuffs.
  - FEET: navy cleats with white stud row; white socks with sky-blue turnover.
  - TAIL: white shorts waistband; tail striped to a point.
- **Palette:** `#7FB6E6` stripe blue · `#FFFFFF` stripe white/socks ·
  `#13294B` collar/number/boots · `#E8B84B` captain armband · `#0A1A33` outline.
- **Distinctness:** The only vertical-stripe pattern and the only armband-led
  "leader" hook; cool blue/white contrasts the warm Striker and the lime Keeper.
  Stripes are a high-recognition kit motif no Skybit skin uses.

---

## 4. THE FLYING DUTCHMAN — `skin_oranje` (design_4)

- **Silhouette:** A near-monochrome **electric-orange** body with two crisp
  **black shoulder stripes** running over the wings (Netherlands '74), a sleek
  swept-back hair crest, and orange boots. The most saturated single-colour
  read on the board — a flying orange comet.
- **Object list + placement:**
  - HEAD: glossy black swept hair crest along the crown; no hat.
  - CHEST/BODY: solid orange jersey; small black lion-crest hint over the heart
    (simple shield shape) + a slim black scoop collar.
  - BACK: black "14" on the back jersey.
  - WINGS: two parallel black stripes flowing from each shoulder down the wing
    (the signature shoulder-stripe tell); orange sleeves.
  - FEET: orange cleats with black studs; black socks with orange turnover;
    shin-guard band.
  - TAIL: black shorts waistband; orange tail.
- **Palette:** `#FF6A00` jersey primary · `#111111` stripes/crest/boots ·
  `#FFA64D` highlight · `#C24A00` body shadow · `#3A1500` outline.
- **Distinctness:** The only all-orange/two-stripe identity and the only
  "shoulder-stripe-over-the-wing" device — it uses the wings themselves as the
  graphic. High-energy warm tone but reads differently from the yellow Striker
  thanks to its monochrome-stripe minimalism. No existing skin is orange.

---

## 5. THE DEFENDER — `skin_blaugrana` (design_5)

- **Silhouette:** Deep **blaugrana** (claret + blue) **vertical halves/stripes**
  with thick **shin guards + rolled socks** dominating the legs and a tough
  short crop on the head. A grounded, stocky "stopper" read with the leg armour
  as the hook.
- **Object list + placement:**
  - HEAD: tight cropped hair + a thin black sweatband low on the brow.
  - CHEST/BODY: claret-and-blue jersey (vertical bands), gold pinstripe between,
    classic round collar in gold.
  - BACK: gold "4" (centre-back number).
  - WINGS: claret sleeves with a blue cuff band; small sponsor-bar hint as a
    single gold rectangle on one chest sleeve.
  - FEET: black cleats with gold studs; **prominent shin guards** as two pale
    rounded plates over the lower legs, wrapped by claret socks rolled at the
    top — the standout tell.
  - TAIL: blue shorts waistband; tail split claret/blue to a point.
- **Palette:** `#7A1F3D` claret · `#15366B` blue · `#E8B84B` gold trim/number ·
  `#1A1A1A` boots · `#0C0820` outline.
- **Distinctness:** The only club (not country) kit, the only claret-and-blue
  pair, and the only concept that makes **leg protection** the hero rather than
  head/chest — the chunky shin-guard plates give it a distinct lower-body
  silhouette. Darker, richer palette balances the four brighter kits.

---

## Ranking (buildability + impact at ~40px)

1. **THE STRIKER** — biggest, simplest, most universally legible "soccer" read;
   yellow/green + a giant number is unmistakable at 40px on any sky. Easiest to
   build (flat blocks + one numeral).
2. **THE GOALKEEPER** — strongest *unique* silhouette (cap + glove-tipped
   wings) and the only one that adds a new shape to the wing flap; acid lime
   pops. Slightly more parts than the Striker.
3. **EL CAPITÁN** — instantly readable stripes + armband; cool palette adds
   variety. Vertical stripes need care so they don't shimmer at 40px, but very
   doable with clean polygon bands.
4. **THE FLYING DUTCHMAN** — gorgeous saturated read and a clever
   wings-as-graphic device; ranked here only because it risks reading close to
   the Striker's warmth from a distance, and the lion-crest hint must stay a
   simple shape or it muddies at 40px.
5. **THE DEFENDER** — the richest, most "premium" look and the only club kit,
   but the most detail-dependent (vertical halves + gold pinstripe + shin
   plates), so it's the hardest to keep crisp at 40px. Best reserved as the
   showpiece if a higher-effort build is wanted.

**Recommended legendary/showpiece pick:** THE GOALKEEPER — the cap and catching
mitts give it spectacle and an animation payoff the others lack.
