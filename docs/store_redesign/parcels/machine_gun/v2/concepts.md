# MACHINE GUN parcel — redesign concepts (v2)

The MACHINE GUN (`parcel_gun`, RARE, 750c) is a ~22px object carried below Pip,
rotating with his bank — the read must survive the rotozoom on DAY **and** NIGHT
sky and stay unmistakably a GUN. The shipped original is a chunky cartoon
tommy-gun (long barrel + slung gunmetal drum + wood grip/stock); these five
explore distinct *kinds* of machine gun, several leaning playful/toy so the item
can read fun rather than violent. Each layers multiple elements (barrel + body +
mag/feed + accent). Numbers map to `design_1 … design_5`.

## 1 · GATLING GUN  (rotary brass)
A hand-cranked rotary gun. **Hero silhouette:** a horizontal CLUSTER of 3–4
parallel barrels projecting forward from a round breech hub, a hand-crank at the
back. **Elements:** the multi-barrel bundle (identity), round brass breech hub,
crank handle, a short frame spar, gunmetal+brass. **Distinctness:** the only
multi-barrel bundle — reads "gatling/rotary" at a glance. **Palette:** gunmetal
`#4A5260`, brass `#C9962E`, brass-hi `#F0C75A`, steel-hi `#AEB8C8`, outline
`#1C1E26`, warm keyline `#D2C8AA`.

## 2 · NERF BLASTER  (foam toy)
A bright toy dart blaster. **Hero silhouette:** a chunky orange shell with a
rotating dart drum showing foam-dart tips at the front and a wide muzzle.
**Elements:** orange body, blue grip/trigger accents, a dart drum with visible
yellow foam-dart tip circles, wide muzzle. **Distinctness:** the only toy/kid
read — bright orange + foam darts, unmistakably non-violent. **Palette:**
nerf-orange `#F07A1E`, nerf-blue `#2E6FC8`, dart-yellow `#F2D23A`, grey
`#8A93A2`, outline `#28221A`.

## 3 · LASER MINIGUN  (sci-fi energy)
A futuristic rotary energy weapon. **Hero silhouette:** a sleek dark body with a
glowing neon-cyan energy coil along the barrel and a bright glowing muzzle core.
**Elements:** dark sleek body, emissive cyan coil/barrel, glowing muzzle, a power
cell, crisp rim light. **Distinctness:** the only emissive/glowing energy weapon
— the glow carries it on night sky. **Palette:** dark `#232A3A`, neon-cyan
`#38E6F0`, cyan-glow `#9CFBFF`, body-hi `#5A6A86`, outline `#10131C`.

## 4 · GOLD DELUXE  (bling flex)
A gold-plated showpiece. **Hero silhouette:** a compact all-gold submachine gun
with a gem inlay and an ivory grip. **Elements:** gold body + barrel, a hint of
engraved scrollwork, a cyan diamond/gem on the side, ivory grip, a sparkle.
**Distinctness:** the only all-gold premium piece — a high-tier flex, distinct
palette. **Palette:** gold `#E3B23C`, gold-hi `#F8E08A`, gold-shade `#9C6E1E`,
gem-cyan `#5CD0E0`, ivory `#EFE6CC`, outline `#3A2A0E`.

## 5 · WATER BLASTER  (super soaker)
A bright water gun. **Hero silhouette:** a teal blaster with a translucent water
RESERVOIR tank on top (visible water line), a pump, and a nozzle. **Elements:**
teal body, translucent blue water tank with a meniscus, pump grip, nozzle,
trigger, a yellow accent. **Distinctness:** the only water/translucent-tank read
— playful and kid-safe, and ties to the WATER BOTTLE already in the roster.
**Palette:** soaker-teal `#1FB6A6`, water-blue `#4FB8E8`, tank-water `#2E8FD0`,
yellow `#F2C53A`, outline `#12303A`.
