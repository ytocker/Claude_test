# ENVELOPE parcel — redesign concepts

The ENVELOPE (`parcel_envelope`, LOW tier, 180c) is the flattest object in the
parcel roster — a ~22px slab carried below Pip, rotating with his bank. The
read must survive the rotozoom at every flight angle on DAY **and** NIGHT sky,
and stay unmistakably an ENVELOPE. Original = "SEALED MAILER": kraft padded
mailer + dark twine X + small red wax seal.

Five distinct envelope identities. Each layers MULTIPLE elements (body + flap +
seal/stamp + accent) so it never reads as a blank card. Numbers map to
`design_1 … design_5`.

## 1 · AIRMAIL  (par-avion)
The classic airmail envelope. **Hero silhouette:** white envelope ringed by the
iconic red+blue dashed candy-stripe border. **Elements:** white body; full-
perimeter red/blue stripe border; small red postage stamp in the top-right
corner; a blue "PAR AVION" label bar lower-left; faint envelope-flap V on top.
**Identity / distinctness:** the red/blue striped border — no other concept has
the candy edge. **Palette:** white `#F2EFE6`, airmail-red `#D2433A`, airmail-
blue `#2E5FA8`, ink `#2A2E3A`, night keyline `#DDE6F0`.

## 2 · LOVE LETTER
A sweet sealed billet-doux. **Hero silhouette:** blush envelope with a glossy
red HEART wax seal dead-centre. **Elements:** pink body; darker rose closing
flap as a downward V; red heart wax seal at the flap point; a tiny ribbon tail;
cream inner edge peeking under the flap. **Identity / distinctness:** the heart
seal on pink — the only warm/romantic read. **Palette:** pink `#F4B8C4`, rose
flap `#D97E92`, heart-red `#D83A4A`, cream `#F6E9DD`, outline `#5A2A33`.

## 3 · ROYAL DECREE  (premium feel)
A wax-sealed noble missive. **Hero silhouette:** cream parchment envelope with
an OVERSIZED gold-rimmed crimson wax crest. **Elements:** parchment body; ornate
pointed flap edged in gold trim; a big crimson wax seal with a gold rim +
embossed crest dot at centre; small gilded corner flourishes. **Identity /
distinctness:** the large ornate gold-rimmed wax crest + gold trim — the richest,
most "valuable" look. **Palette:** parchment `#EFE2C0`, gold `#E3B23C`, gold-hi
`#F6D77A`, crimson wax `#A8243A`, ink `#3A2E1E`.

## 4 · POST OFFICE  (cancellation stamp)
A franked vintage postal envelope. **Hero silhouette:** manila envelope struck
by a bold round rubber-stamp cancellation mark. **Elements:** manila body; a
circular ink cancellation stamp (two concentric rings + a star/bars) across the
top-right; a small perforated postage stamp opposite; 2–3 faint address ruling
lines. **Identity / distinctness:** the inked circular cancellation rings — the
only "postmarked / in-transit" read. **Palette:** manila `#D9B97E`, manila-shade
`#B8965A`, stamp-ink `#34302A`, postage-red `#C0392B`, outline `#2C2620`.

## 5 · PAPER PLANE
A letter folded mid-flight into a paper dart. **Hero silhouette:** a crisp white
origami dart with clean crease lines. **Elements:** folded-paper triangular dart
body; sharp fold-crease lines down the spine + wing; a cool-blue under-shadow
for origami depth; a small dashed flight-path arc trailing behind. **Identity /
distinctness:** the folded triangular dart + creases — the only one that breaks
the rectangular envelope silhouette (a letter in motion). **Palette:** paper-
white `#EDEDE6`, cool fold-shadow `#B4BDCB`, crease `#8893A4`, sky-accent
`#4A90D9`, outline `#2E343E`.
