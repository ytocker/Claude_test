# FINEST WHISKEY — new SECRET parcel concepts

A premium, top-tier SECRET parcel (masked ??? in the store until bought, like
SNOWGLOBE) — the finest whiskey bottle Pip carries below him. A ~22px object
rotating with his bank: the read must survive the rotozoom on DAY **and** NIGHT
sky and stay unmistakably a fancy BOTTLE OF WHISKEY. The shared identity across
all five is the AMBER liquid + a glassy bottle silhouette + a premium tell
(label / seal / facets). Each is a distinct *kind* of fine-whiskey vessel so the
five read apart at a glance. Each layers multiple elements (bottle body + neck +
cap/stopper + amber fill + a gold/label accent). Numbers map to `design_1 … 5`.

## 1 · CRYSTAL DECANTER
A cut-crystal spirits decanter. **Hero silhouette:** a squat wide-shouldered
DECANTER with a fat faceted KNOB stopper, filled with amber whiskey. **Elements:**
faceted glass body (diamond-cut sheen lines), round knob stopper, amber fill with
a bright meniscus, a sharp glass glint. **Distinctness:** the only squat faceted
decanter + knob stopper — heavy crystal, not a tall bottle. **Palette:** glass
`#CFE0E6`, amber `#C8791E`, amber-hi `#F0B45A`, gold `#E3B23C`, outline `#2A2418`.

## 2 · SCOTCH FIFTH  (labeled bottle)
The classic single-malt bottle. **Hero silhouette:** a tall round-shouldered
bottle with a cream/GOLD LABEL across the middle, a foil-wrapped neck, and amber
inside. **Elements:** tall glass body, round shoulder, foil neck wrap, a
cream+gold rectangular label (the brand read), amber fill. **Distinctness:** the
only tall labeled scotch bottle — the label band is its tell. **Palette:** glass
`#9FC2C2`, amber `#B86A18`, label-cream `#F2E6C8`, gold `#E3B23C`, outline
`#241C12`.

## 3 · SQUARE BOURBON  (black & gold)
A square Tennessee-whiskey bottle. **Hero silhouette:** a SQUARE-shouldered bottle
with a BLACK label + gold trim and a short black cap. **Elements:** square glass
body, black centre label with a thin gold border + gold band, black screw cap,
amber fill peeking around the label. **Distinctness:** the only square black-label
bottle — boxy, monochrome+gold. **Palette:** glass `#A8B6A0`, amber `#A85E16`,
label-black `#1E1E22`, gold `#E3B23C`, outline `#16160E`.

## 4 · GOLD RESERVE  (wax-sealed flex)
An ornate gilded reserve. **Hero silhouette:** a bottle cased in GOLD with a red
WAX SEAL over the cork and a small ribbon medallion. **Elements:** gold-banded
glass body, gold neck foil, a crimson wax seal blob on the cork, a tiny hanging
ribbon medallion, amber fill. **Distinctness:** the only gold-cased wax-sealed
flex — the richest, most "finest" read. **Palette:** gold `#E3B23C`, gold-hi
`#F8E08A`, glass-amber `#B86A18`, wax-red `#A8243A`, outline `#2A1E0E`.

## 5 · CASKED DRAM  (barrel-aged)
A cask-aged dram. **Hero silhouette:** an amber bottle with a CORK stopper seated
in a small WOODEN barrel-stave cradle. **Elements:** rounded amber bottle, pale
cork top, a curved wood-stave cradle with iron hoop bands under/behind it, a
small aged paper tag. **Distinctness:** the only bottle-in-a-wood-cradle — the
barrel staves are its tell. **Palette:** amber `#BE7320`, amber-hi `#F0B45A`,
cork `#C9A06A`, wood `#7A4F2E`, iron-hoop `#5A5E66`, outline `#241A10`.
