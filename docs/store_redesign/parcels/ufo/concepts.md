# MINI UFO (`parcel_ufo`) — Redesign Concepts

Five from-scratch saucer concepts, each visually distinct from each other and
from the current chrome-disc + teal-dome + hard-beam design. Render target:
22x22px (built at 44x44, 2x downscale). Read-critical zone is the LOWER 70% of
the sprite (top ~25-30% is eaten by Pip's belly/tail). Dark 1-2px outline on the
main silhouette is mandatory. Numbers map to design_1..5.

Research touchstones: 1950s B-movie pulp posters, Close Encounters bubble-dome
ships, ancient-astronaut gold discs, cyberpunk neon signage, disco/mirror-ball
saucers. (Sources listed at bottom.)

---

## 1. RETRO ROCKET-POP
**Concept:** A candy-coloured 1950s B-movie saucer — cheerful pulp, not chrome.
A stubby cherry-red hull with a cream porthole band, like a flying diner.
**Shape/elements:** Wide flat disc, but SHORTER and rounder than the current one
(rx:rx ~ 16x7). A single continuous CREAM horizontal band across the disc's
equator holds a row of 3-4 chunky round PORTHOLES — the porthole row is the tell
and lives dead-centre in the lower-70% read zone. Small squashed clear dome on
top (mostly obscured, fine). Little tripod landing nub peeks below.
**Palette:** `#E63A46` cherry hull / `#F6E7C8` cream band / `#3AA6C4` porthole
glass / `#2A1418` outline / `#FFD25A` warm porthole glow.
**Beam/effect:** No column beam — instead the 3-4 portholes each emit a soft
warm-yellow glow (`#FFD25A`), so the tell is a "row of glowing windows" reading
as an occupied ship. Premium feel comes from the cream band + saturated red.
**Distinct vs others:** The only warm/red saucer and the only one whose signature
is a horizontal PORTHOLE ROW rather than a beam or a big dome.

## 2. BUBBLE SCOUT
**Concept:** A cute Close-Encounters "little scout" — a tiny hull dwarfed by an
OVERSIZED clear glass bubble-dome with a big-eyed grey alien pilot inside.
**Shape/elements:** Thin slate saucer disc (rx ~15, ry ~5) acting mostly as a
base ring; the dome is the hero — a large bright glass hemisphere with a bold
white crescent highlight. Inside, a simple silhouette: two big black almond eyes
+ round head. Because carry buries the top, the dome sits LOW and forward so the
eyes clear Pip's belly. Green under-rim glow anchors the base in the read zone.
**Palette:** `#B7C6D0` slate hull / `#CFF3FF` glass dome / `#8CF08A` alien-green
under-glow / `#1B2730` outline / `#0E1418` alien-eye black.
**Beam/effect:** No column — a soft GREEN halo pulses from the underside rim
(`#8CF08A`), sci-fi "hovering" glow. The pilot's eyes are the premium charm hook.
**Distinct vs others:** The only character-driven saucer (a visible alien pilot)
and the only one where a giant bubble-dome, not the disc, is the silhouette.

## 3. GOLDEN GLYPH DISC
**Concept:** An ancient-astronaut artifact — a hammered GOLD saucer etched with
glowing turquoise alien glyphs, like a relic that hums with power.
**Shape/elements:** Classic wide saucer (rx ~17, ry ~6) in warm brushed gold with
a low ceremonial gold dome. The lower disc face carries 3-4 simple GLOWING GLYPH
marks (dot, chevron, ring, bar) in turquoise, spaced along the belly where they
stay visible. Value structure: bright gold crown, deep bronze underside, glyphs
pop as the brightest cool accent. Slightly ridged rim reads as "carved."
**Palette:** `#F4C544` bright gold / `#B07A1E` deep bronze / `#2EE6C6` glyph
turquoise / `#2A1B08` outline / `#FFF0B0` gold crown catch.
**Beam/effect:** No projected beam — the GLYPHS glow with a soft turquoise halo
that faintly pulses, and a thin turquoise energy line traces the lower rim. The
gold+turquoise contrast is inherently premium/"legendary artifact."
**Distinct vs others:** The only gold/warm-metal saucer and the only one whose
signature is glowing ETCHED GLYPHS on the hull face.

## 4. NEON NIGHT-DINER
**Concept:** A cyberpunk saucer built like a glowing neon sign — dark hull, bright
electric outlines, magenta/cyan tube-light that pops hardest against night sky.
**Shape/elements:** Sleek angular disc (rx ~17, ry ~5), near-black hull so it
reads as a "canvas" for light. The silhouette is drawn in glowing NEON TUBE lines:
a cyan rim stroke around the whole disc, a magenta stroke on the equator seam, and
a small magenta-outlined dome. A dotted chase-light ring (alternating cyan/magenta
pips) rings the lower rim — the signature. Dark hull keeps top-obscured area
irrelevant; all read lives in the glowing lower edge.
**Palette:** `#141020` near-black hull / `#00E5FF` neon cyan / `#FF3DCB` neon
magenta / `#7A00FF` violet inner glow / `#0A0610` outline.
**Beam/effect:** A short, wide DOWNWARD neon cone drawn as two bright cyan edge
lines with a translucent violet fill between them (`#7A00FF`) — reads as a light
beam made of neon, not white light. Every stroke carries an outer glow for the
premium sign look.
**Distinct vs others:** The only dark-hull / neon-line saucer, built as glowing
outlines rather than solid metal, and the only night-sky-optimised design.

## 5. DISCO MIRROR-BALL SAUCER
**Concept:** A party saucer whose hull is a faceted MIRROR-BALL disc throwing
coloured sparkle dots — playful, premium, unmistakably festive.
**Shape/elements:** Wide disc (rx ~17, ry ~6) rendered as a grid of small diamond
FACETS in cool silver-violet, each facet a slightly different value so it reads as
mirrored. A small mirrored dome on top. The tell is the SPARKLE: a scatter of tiny
2px coloured glints (pink/gold/cyan) sitting on the brightest facets across the
lower disc face, plus a couple of star-glints floating just off the rim.
**Palette:** `#C9C3E8` mirror silver-violet / `#8A7FD0` facet shadow / `#FF6FD8`
pink glint / `#66E0FF` cyan glint / `#20182E` outline (gold glint `#FFD75A` as 5th
accent).
**Beam/effect:** Instead of a beam, a fan of 3-4 short COLOURED LIGHT-RAYS spills
from the underside (pink/cyan/gold), like a mirror-ball scattering light — reads
as premium sparkle in the lower zone. Faint animated-feel twinkle via alternating
glint brightness.
**Distinct vs others:** The only faceted/mirror-ball saucer and the only one whose
signature is multicoloured scattered SPARKLE rather than a single-hue glow or beam.

## Ranking / notes
- **Strongest overall:** #2 BUBBLE SCOUT (charm + a character read no other parcel
  has) and #3 GOLDEN GLYPH DISC (most obviously "premium 7000-coin" feel).
- **Best day-sky read:** #1 RETRO ROCKET-POP (warm red vs blue sky, bold band).
- **Best night-sky flex:** #4 NEON NIGHT-DINER.
- **Riskiest at 22px:** #5 DISCO (facet grid can mush) and #2's pilot eyes — both
  need the tell weighted low and chunky; flag for the design loop.
