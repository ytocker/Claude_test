# Soccer Player Parcel — 5 Concepts

Themed around a **professional soccer player** (NOT a ball). Each is a single
bold 22×22 silhouette, drawable in pure Pygame (polygons, circles, lines,
gradients) and legible when tilted during flight. Numbers map to design_1…5.

Constraints honoured: distinct from existing parcels (envelope, burlap sack,
takeout pail, jam jar, picnic basket, dim sum steamer, message bottle, hot-air
balloon, treasure chest, paper lantern, genie flask, mini UFO, comet,
snowglobe). No ball.

---

## 1. GOLDEN BOOT  — `design_1`  ★ top pick

1. **Name:** GOLDEN BOOT
2. **Silhouette:** A side-on cleat — high pointed toe sloping down to a flat
   sole, raised heel block at the back; the most iconic single soccer object
   that isn't a ball. Fills the 22×22 diagonally for maximum size.
3. **Key features:** Row of conical studs along the bottom edge; angled lace
   panel of 3–4 V-shaped lace lines on the upper; defined ankle collar opening
   at the top-rear; bright specular highlight streak across the toe to read as
   polished gold metal.
4. **Palette:** `#FFD24A` (gold body), `#C8901E` (shadow/underside),
   `#FFF3B0` (highlight streak), `#6B4A12` (stud + outline), `#FFFFFF` (lace).
5. **Distinctness:** No existing parcel is footwear or a trophy form; the gold
   metallic + stud row is unmistakable soccer at a glance and doubles as a
   "top scorer" prestige cue. Strongest single-shape soccer read.

---

## 2. CAPTAIN'S ARMBAND  — `design_2`  ★ second pick

1. **Name:** CAPTAIN BAND
2. **Silhouette:** A short fat curved tube/cuff — a horizontal banded cylinder
   with a clear hollow opening, like a rolled wristband floating below Pip.
   Compact bold blob shape reads instantly even tilted.
3. **Key features:** Bold tricolour horizontal stripes (the classic look); a
   big black **"C"** centred on a white middle band; a small dark
   hook-and-loop tab at one end; subtle inner-rim shadow to sell the tube
   opening.
4. **Palette:** `#E23B2E` (red stripe), `#2E5FE2` (blue stripe), `#FFFFFF`
   (centre band), `#1A1A1A` ("C" + tab), `#B02018` (inner shadow).
5. **Distinctness:** A wearable kit accessory with a literal letterform — no
   other parcel carries text or the leadership/captain symbolism. High-contrast
   stripes pop against both day and night skies.

---

## 3. SHIN GUARD  — `design_3`

1. **Name:** SHIN GUARD
2. **Silhouette:** A rounded vertical shield/teardrop — wide curved top
   tapering to a narrower rounded bottom, gently dished (convex). A clean
   single armour-plate outline that fills the canvas vertically.
3. **Key features:** Central raised ridge running top-to-bottom; two strap slots
   or a single elastic strap crossing horizontally; a small jersey-number "10"
   or star badge near the top; soft top-lit gradient to show the curved plastic.
4. **Palette:** `#2BB36B` (guard body), `#1C7A48` (dish shadow),
   `#EAF7EF` (top highlight), `#FFFFFF` (strap), `#0E3D24` (outline + number).
5. **Distinctness:** A protective gear shape unlike any container parcel; the
   shield silhouette + jersey number says "player kit" without a ball, and the
   green keeps it distinct from the red/blue armband.

---

## 4. KEEPER GLOVE  — `design_4`

1. **Name:** KEEPER GLOVE
2. **Silhouette:** A chunky padded mitten — four blocky fingers up, a thumb out
   to one side, fat rounded backhand. A bold "hand" shape that is instantly
   recognisable and unique in silhouette terms among the parcels.
3. **Key features:** Latex palm panel in a contrasting colour with finger-pad
   dots/grip texture; a wide wrist strap cuff at the bottom; visible finger
   segment seams; a punch-ready slight curl to the fingers.
4. **Palette:** `#FF7A1A` (backhand), `#1E1E22` (latex palm), `#FFFFFF`
   (cuff strap), `#FFD24A` (grip dots), `#9E430A` (outline/shadow).
5. **Distinctness:** The only parcel that reads as a worn hand; goalkeeper
   identity is unique to soccer and totally different from footwear/armband.
   Orange backhand is a strong day-and-night value anchor.

---

## 5. REFEREE WHISTLE  — `design_5`

1. **Name:** PEA WHISTLE
2. **Silhouette:** A fat teardrop/pebble body with a flat mouthpiece spout off
   one side and a small ring/lanyard loop at the top — a compact rounded object
   with one clear protrusion that stays readable when tilted.
3. **Key features:** Glossy chrome body with a curved top highlight; the
   barrel-shaped "pea" chamber bulge; a short ridged mouthpiece; a tiny rope
   loop + bead at the top (hints the lanyard); a slot/air-hole detail.
4. **Palette:** `#C0C6CC` (chrome body), `#7E868E` (shadow), `#F2F5F8`
   (highlight), `#1A1A1A` (mouth slot + loop), `#E0A53B` (lanyard bead).
5. **Distinctness:** The official/match-day side of soccer — no parcel is a
   metal instrument; the spout+loop combo gives a one-of-a-kind silhouette and
   a fun "ref" character beat distinct from the four player-kit items above.

---

## Ranking & rationale

1. **GOLDEN BOOT** — the single most iconic non-ball soccer object; gold +
   studs is unmistakable and carries prestige. Best 40px read of the set.
2. **CAPTAIN BAND** — the letterform "C" and tricolour stripes give instant
   theme + meaning; punchy colour, very buildable.
3. **SHIN GUARD** — clean shield shape, great value structure, clearly kit.
4. **KEEPER GLOVE** — strong unique silhouette, slightly busier to draw small.
5. **PEA WHISTLE** — charming and distinct, but smallest/most detail-dependent,
   so ranked last for the 22×22 read.

Spread of identities: trophy/boot, wearable accessory, protective plate,
goalkeeper, official — varied palettes (gold, red-blue, green, orange, chrome)
so no two parcels clash or read alike in the store grid.
