# PARROT CHILD parcel — concepts (baby Pip carried instead of the parcel)

A new parcel: instead of a gift box, Pip carries his CHILD — a small baby parrot
that reads "more or less like the original Pip, but smaller". A ~22px object
below Pip, rotating with his bank: the read must survive the rotozoom on DAY
**and** NIGHT and stay unmistakably a tiny PARROT that's clearly Pip's offspring
(same red body / blue wing / yellow accents / dark face family as the parent).
The reference parent art is `game/parrot.py` (`_build_frame` / the scaled
`_build_frame_scaled`, and the `BIRD_RED / BIRD_WING / BIRD_TIP / BIRD_BELLY /
BIRD_BEAK` palette) — match that palette so the baby reads as Pip's kin.

Five distinct baby reads, all the same red macaw family. Numbers map to
`design_1 … 5`.

## 1 · MINI-PIP  (faithful small copy)
The closest to "the original, smaller". **Silhouette:** a scaled-down faithful
Pip — same macaw body/wing/beak shapes and palette, sat upright looking up at the
parent, NO sunglasses (a baby), big bright eye. **Distinctness:** the literal
chibi-Pip; reads as the parent shrunk. **Palette:** Pip's own (`BIRD_RED`,
`BIRD_WING` blue, `BIRD_TIP` yellow, `BIRD_BEAK`, dark eye).

## 2 · DOWNY CHICK  (max-cute chibi)
A round fluffball baby. **Silhouette:** oversized round head, tiny body, a wispy
3-strand head TUFT, stubby half-formed wings, big sparkly eye. Softer, downier
red with paler belly fluff. **Distinctness:** the head-tuft + big-head chibi
proportions — the cutest, roundest read. **Palette:** Pip red + a paler downy
belly, yellow tuft tips.

## 3 · EGG HATCHLING  (just hatched)
The baby breaking out of its shell. **Silhouette:** a cracked cream EGG (lower
half a jagged-rim shell) with the baby parrot's head + tiny wings poking up out
of it. **Distinctness:** the only one with the egg shell — "newly hatched".
**Palette:** cream/white egg shell + Pip-red baby head + yellow beak.

## 4 · SLEEPY BABY  (curled asleep)
A napping chick. **Silhouette:** a curled-up round baby parrot, eyes closed (a
soft curved line), beak tucked, a tiny "z z" sleep mark rising. A calm rounded
ball of red feather with the blue wing folded over like a blanket. **Distinctness:**
the only closed-eye sleeping pose + the z's. **Palette:** Pip red/blue, softened.

## 5 · PEEKER  (big-eyed, reaching up)
The baby reaching for its parent. **Silhouette:** a small parrot with HUGE shiny
eyes looking up, tiny wings raised/outstretched toward Pip, beak open in a little
chirp. Lively and alert. **Distinctness:** the raised wings + oversized eyes +
open chirping beak — the most animated, "pick me up" read. **Palette:** Pip red /
blue wing / yellow, bright white eye-shine.
