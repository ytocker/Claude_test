# WATER BOTTLE parcel — redesign concepts (v2)

The WATER BOTTLE (`parcel_water`, COMMON, 300c) is a ~22px object carried below
Pip, rotating with his bank — the read must survive the rotozoom on DAY **and**
NIGHT sky and stay unmistakably a DRINK CONTAINER. The shipped original is a
clear sports SQUEEZE bottle (straight walls, red cap, high water line). These
five explore distinct *kinds* of water/drink vessel — each a clearly different
SILHOUETTE so the swap reads at a glance. Each layers multiple elements
(body + cap/lid + water/label + accent). Numbers map to `design_1 … design_5`.

## 1 · DISPOSABLE BOTTLE  (supermarket PET)
The iconic single-use water bottle. **Hero silhouette:** a tall clear PET bottle
with a ribbed lower body, a small blue screw cap, and a white/blue paper LABEL
band across the middle. **Elements:** clear blue-tinted body, ribbed base lines,
blue cap, label band (the brand read), blue water. **Distinctness:** the only one
with a mid-body label band + ribbed PET base. **Palette:** clear-blue `#BFE3F2`,
water `#3E9AD6`, cap-blue `#2E6FC8`, label-white `#F2F4F8`, outline `#1E3550`.

## 2 · INSULATED TUMBLER  (hydro-flask)
The trendy reusable bottle. **Hero silhouette:** a chunky straight-walled MATTE
pastel body with a black flip-straw LID and a small carry LOOP on top.
**Elements:** wide pastel-teal body, black lid cap, carry loop, a subtle side
sticker dot. **Distinctness:** the only chunky matte body + black lid + loop;
opaque, not see-through. **Palette:** pastel-teal `#5CC9C0`, teal-shade
`#2E9A92`, lid-black `#2A2E36`, loop-grey `#6A727E`, outline `#15302E`.

## 3 · HIKER CANTEEN  (field flask)
A vintage scout canteen. **Hero silhouette:** a rounded, slightly flattened
canteen in canvas-OLIVE with a steel screw cap on a short neck and a STRAP loop.
**Elements:** round olive canvas body, stitched seam line, steel cap + neck,
strap loop, a metal rivet. **Distinctness:** the only round/flattened canteen
silhouette with a strap. **Palette:** olive `#7E8A4E`, olive-shade `#565E32`,
steel-cap `#AEB6C0`, strap `#4A3A28`, outline `#2A2E1C`.

## 4 · COCONUT DRINK  (tropical)
A beach refresher (ties to the lagoon theme). **Hero silhouette:** a brown
COCONUT with a cut-open pale top, a striped STRAW poking out, a tiny cocktail
UMBRELLA, and a green leaf. **Elements:** coconut sphere, pale cut flesh, striped
straw, mini umbrella, leaf sprig. **Distinctness:** the only round tropical drink
— the umbrella + straw are unmistakable. **Palette:** coconut-brown `#7A4F2E`,
coconut-shade `#563418`, flesh `#F0E6D2`, straw-red `#E0483E`, umbrella-pink
`#F08FB0`, leaf-green `#3FA35A`, outline `#2A1A0E`.

## 5 · GALLON JUG  (gym water)
A big workout water jug. **Hero silhouette:** a large translucent JUG with a side
carry HANDLE, measurement TICK marks up the side, and a coloured sport flip cap.
**Elements:** wide translucent body, side handle loop (with a hole), tick marks,
yellow sport cap, blue water. **Distinctness:** the only one with a side handle
loop + measurement ticks; the bulkiest body. **Palette:** jug-blue `#7EC8E8`,
water `#3E9AD6`, handle-frame `#2E6FC8`, cap-accent `#F2C53A`, tick-white
`#EAF2F8`, outline `#1E3550`.
