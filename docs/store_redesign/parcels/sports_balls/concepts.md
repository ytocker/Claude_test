# SPORTS BALLS parcels — concepts (one ball per common sport)

Five new parcels, each a classic SPORTS BALL Pip carries below him — the most
common / instantly-recognizable sports. A ~22px object rotating with his bank:
the read must survive the rotozoom on DAY **and** NIGHT and stay unmistakably
the right ball. Each ball's IDENTITY rides on a bold colour + a signature seam
pattern (kept to a FEW committed marks at 22px, never a fussy grid). Reuse the
shared parcel scaffold (44px work surface → smoothscale to 22; baked dark
OUTLINE first for DAY; a keyline rim for NIGHT; held off the edges). Numbers map
to `design_1 … 5`.

## 1 · SOCCER BALL  (football)
The universal one. **Silhouette:** a white sphere with the classic black
PENTAGON patches (one centred + partial ones around it). **Identity:** the
black-on-white pentagon pattern. **Palette:** white `#F4F5F7`, panel-black
`#22242A`, shade `#C8CCD4`, outline `#1A1C22`. Strong value contrast = survives
grayscale.

## 2 · BASKETBALL
**Silhouette:** an orange sphere crossed by the black SEAM lines — one vertical,
one horizontal, plus the two curved side seams. **Identity:** orange + the black
seam cross. **Palette:** basketball-orange `#E07A22`, orange-shade `#A8551A`,
seam-black `#201A14`, highlight `#F2A24E`, outline `#16120C`.

## 3 · TENNIS BALL
**Silhouette:** a neon yellow-green fuzzy sphere with the single white CURVED
seam (the curved hourglass line). **Identity:** the neon colour + the white
curve. **Palette:** tennis-yellow `#D2E84A`, yellow-shade `#9FB832`, seam-white
`#F4F7E8`, fuzz-hi `#E8F472`, outline `#3A4414`.

## 4 · BASEBALL
**Silhouette:** a white sphere with the two red curved STITCH seams (the dotted
red stitches along two facing curves). **Identity:** white + the red stitching.
**Palette:** white `#F4F2EC`, shade `#D2CEC2`, stitch-red `#D23A30`, outline
`#2A2620`. (Distinct from soccer: red stitches, no black panels.)

## 5 · AMERICAN FOOTBALL
The only non-round one — silhouette distinctness. **Silhouette:** a brown
prolate (pointed oval) ball with the white END STRIPES near each tip and the
white LACES across the centre. **Identity:** the oval shape + laces. **Palette:**
pigskin-brown `#7A4A24`, brown-shade `#542F14`, lace-white `#F2EEE2`, highlight
`#9E6A38`, outline `#241408`.
