# SUN — secret store item, born from the original pufferfish star-burst

**Origin.** The original `skin_pufferfish` was a golden ball ringed by a
symmetric radial needle-halo with a friendly face — it read so strongly as a SUN
that we redesigned the puffer into a real fish. This item reclaims that art on
purpose: a **secret/masked flyer** (`"secret": True`, ??? in the store like the
UFO / toaster / piñata flyers) that IS a sun. The puffer's star-burst is the
base; each concept leans fully into "sun" — full even radial rays, a warm
golden core, and a face — and varies the sun's *personality*.

**Carried over from the puffer base** (`tools/sun_candidates/_shared.py`, copied
from the original `animal_pufferfish` helpers): `_radial_body` (core→mid→edge
sphere), `_spike_ring` (two-tone radial rays — now the sun's corona), `_eye`,
`_shade`, and the pulse gag — the puffer's `_inflate` becomes a **SHINE pulse**
(rays flare + the whole disc brightens on the down-stroke, settles on the up).

All read at the 40px in-motion truth read, day AND night (a glowing disc must
still pop against a bright daytime sky). Numbers map to design_1…design_5.

---

## 1. CLASSIC SUNFACE — the smiling sun  `skin_sun`
The literal, iconic sun: the puffer star-burst made symmetric and friendly.
- **Hero silhouette:** a golden disc with a FULL even halo of **alternating
  long/short triangular rays** all the way around — the universal sun shape.
- **Parts:** warm gold→amber radial body, a clean white-gold core, the classic
  cartoon **sun-face** (the puffer's friendly round eyes + a cheerful smile +
  rosy cheeks), even 12+12 long/short ray ring with a 2-tone value step.
- **Palette:** `#FFD23E` gold, `#FFE89A` core, `#E0962C` ray base, `#FFF0B0` ray
  tip, `#C8721E` rim, `#7A3E1E` face.
- **Distinctness:** the plain happy emoji-sun — the safest, most universally
  readable; symmetry is the point (the opposite of the fish fix).

## 2. BLAZING SUN — fierce radiant  `skin_sun_blaze`
A hot, powerful sun with licking flame rays and a fierce face.
- **Hero silhouette:** wavy **flame-tongue rays** (curved, sweeping the same
  rotational direction) around a white-hot core — a sun in motion, not static.
- **Parts:** layered corona (long curved flames + a short inner ring), a
  white-hot `#FFF6D8` core fading to deep orange-red rim, a determined/fierce
  face (angled brows + a confident grin), a faint outer glow.
- **Palette:** `#FFF6D8` white-hot, `#FFB02E` orange, `#FF6A1E` deep flame,
  `#C43A12` red rim, `#3A1A0E` brow.
- **Distinctness:** the only flame-ray (curved) sun + fierce face; dynamic and
  intense vs #1's calm symmetry.

## 3. SYNTHWAVE SUN — retro sunset  `skin_sun_retro`
An 80s vaporwave sun: a banded gradient disc with the iconic horizontal slits.
- **Hero silhouette:** a disc filled with a **vertical magenta→orange→yellow
  gradient** crossed by 3–4 dark horizontal **slit bands** (the retro sunset
  cliché), rays only fanning off the UPPER half (a rising sun).
- **Parts:** banded gradient body, upper-half triangular rays in hot pink/orange,
  the lower edge sliced by gaps, a cool minimal face (or faceless for the pure
  retro logo read — small calm eyes).
- **Palette:** `#FF3DAE` magenta, `#FF7A3D` orange, `#FFD23E` yellow,
  `#2A1233` slit shadow, `#FFE6F2` glow.
- **Distinctness:** the only gradient/banded + half-halo sun; a graphic-design
  sunset, not a cartoon sun — strong secret-tier novelty.

## 4. KAWAII SUN — chibi cutie  `skin_sun_kawaii`
Maximum charm: a soft pastel sun with huge sparkly eyes.
- **Hero silhouette:** a plump golden disc with **soft rounded stubby rays**
  (gentle scallops, not sharp needles) and a big-eyed adorable face.
- **Parts:** pastel gold body, big sparkly r5 eyes + a tiny smile + big round
  blush, rounded bobble rays, a couple of sparkle stars off the corona.
- **Palette:** `#FFDD66` pastel gold, `#FFF1C2` core, `#FFC04D` ray, `#FF9EB0`
  blush, `#FFFBEA` sparkle.
- **Distinctness:** the only soft-rayed kawaii sun; cuteness-forward, the cuddly
  counterpart to #2's fierce.

## 5. SOLAR DEITY — radiant showpiece  `skin_sun_deity` *(legendary-tier)*
The flex secret item: a majestic layered sun with a double corona and glow.
- **Hero silhouette:** a faceted gold **core ringed by concentric bands**, a
  **double corona** (a long outer ray ring + a short inner ring offset), plus a
  few wispy solar-flare licks — the grandest, brightest disc.
- **Parts:** concentric ring core (gold→white-hot), long+short two-ring corona
  with a strong value step, a serene majestic face (calm eyes, subtle smile),
  an animated shimmer/glow that pulses with the shine gag.
- **Palette:** `#FFF4C8` white-hot core, `#FFD23E` gold, `#F0A028` deep gold,
  `#FFB94A` flare, `#B5701A` rim, soft `#FFE9A0` bloom.
- **Distinctness:** the only double-corona + concentric-core + glowing legendary
  sun; the showpiece that earns a top secret-tier price.
