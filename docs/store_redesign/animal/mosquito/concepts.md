# MOSQUITO — Skin Concepts

One-button Skybit flyer, side-on, ~64×84 canvas, must read at 40px in motion.
Tier: animal, price band ~480–700 coins (between OWL and EAGLE).

**Non-negotiable read at 40px:** the *proboscis* (forward needle) + *dangly
legs* (low six-line rig) are the mosquito's two silhouette tells. Every concept
below leads with a long forward spike and a spindly trailing leg-cluster so the
creature is unmistakable even before palette registers. The wing is the flap
(4 poses); the striped abdomen carries the color story.

Design maps: numbers below map to `design_1 … design_5`. Ranked best-first.

---

## 1. MIDNIGHT WHINE  ·  `skin_mosquito` (default id) — RANK 1
- **Tier:** late-game (hero everyday skin, the "definitive" mosquito).
- **Hero silhouette:** humpbacked teardrop thorax with a long straight
  proboscis lancing forward and a fan of six thin dangling legs trailing low —
  the classic pest-on-a-lampshade shape, instantly a mosquito.
- **Signature feature:** oil-slick **iridescent wing** — a single narrow
  translucent blade with a blue→violet→teal gradient sheen that catches a
  bright specular streak on the down-pose. Deep-blue body reads as a crisp dark
  silhouette against day sky and glows subtly against night.
- **Object list + placement:**
  - Proboscis: long tapered forward needle, near-black with a pale highlight
    ridge; two short palps flanking it (the "three tubes" tell).
  - Compound eye: one big rounded dark-magenta eye with a tiny bright catchlight.
  - Thorax: arched round hump, deep indigo with a top rim-light.
  - Abdomen: pointed, banded indigo/steel stripes tapering to a tip.
  - Legs: six hair-thin bent lines, jointed mid-way, dangling and splayed.
  - Wing: narrow iridescent blade, the animating flap element.
- **Palette:** `#12132B` (near-black indigo body), `#2B3A7A` (mid indigo),
  `#5EC8E5` (iridescent teal sheen), `#8E3FD6` (violet wing shimmer),
  `#C0466F` (compound-eye magenta).
- **Distinctness:** the grounded, realistic, "actual pest" mosquito — dark and
  elegant where the others go loud; sells itself on the wing's oil-slick shimmer.

## 2. AMBER RELIC  ·  `skin_mosquito_amber` — RANK 2 (LEGENDARY showpiece)
- **Tier:** legendary — the spectacle flex.
- **Hero silhouette:** same unmistakable mosquito frame (forward proboscis +
  dangling legs) but rendered as if **suspended inside a glowing amber teardrop**
  — a warm golden aura hugs the whole body outline.
- **Signature feature:** a soft radial **amber glow halo** baked behind the
  sprite with faint frozen "flow lines" and 2–3 tiny drifting bubble specks;
  the creature itself is a darkened fossil silhouette with honey rim-light. Legs
  and proboscis catch a bright caramel edge so they still read at 40px.
- **Object list + placement:**
  - Amber field: layered radial gradient (bright core → deep amber edge) as a
    rounded-lozenge glow behind the body; a couple of pale bubble dots + a
    frozen streak highlight.
  - Body/thorax/abdomen: dark sepia fossil silhouette with a honey top rim.
  - Proboscis + palps: dark needle with a sharp gold highlight line.
  - Eye: amber-on-brown, low contrast (it's "preserved," not alive).
  - Wings: translucent, drawn as thin veined ghosts tinted gold.
  - Legs: six curled dark filaments haloed by the glow.
- **Palette:** `#3A1E08` (fossil dark), `#8A4B12` (mid sepia), `#E8A22C`
  (amber core), `#FFD873` (honey highlight), `#FFF3C9` (bubble/spark glint).
  Glow note: additive radial amber halo + slow shimmer on the bubbles.
- **Distinctness:** the only concept where the *medium* is the star — a warm
  backlit gemstone against cool skies; pure prehistoric-luxury spectacle.

## 3. NEON SKEETER  ·  `skin_mosquito_neon` — RANK 3
- **Tier:** late-game.
- **Hero silhouette:** sleek dark body with **electric outline glow**; the
  proboscis and every leg render as bright neon strokes so the spindly rig
  becomes the light show — a "circuit-bug" reading strongly at 40px.
- **Signature feature:** high-saturation **cyan/magenta neon linework** on a
  charcoal body — legs and proboscis are glowing tubes, wing is a hot-pink
  translucent panel with a scanline shimmer.
- **Object list + placement:**
  - Proboscis: bright cyan glowing needle with a soft bloom.
  - Legs: six magenta neon strokes, each with a faint outer glow, dangling low.
  - Thorax/abdomen: charcoal hump; abdomen banded in alternating cyan/magenta
    glow stripes (keeps the mosquito banding, made electric).
  - Eye: a single bright lime dot with bloom.
  - Wing: translucent hot-pink blade with a light diagonal shimmer line.
- **Palette:** `#171325` (charcoal base), `#17E8E8` (cyan glow), `#FF2FA6`
  (magenta glow), `#B6FF3C` (lime eye), `#5A2A8F` (deep violet shade).
- **Distinctness:** the loud arcade/synthwave pick — all glowing linework where
  #1 is matte-dark and #2 is warm; reads as a tiny flying neon sign.

## 4. VAMPING FANG  ·  `skin_mosquito_vamp` — RANK 4
- **Tier:** late-game.
- **Hero silhouette:** hunched Aedes-style **upside-down-V posture** with a
  fat blood-filled abdomen and a menacing forward proboscis — the "just bit
  you" gothic mosquito; legs dangle like a spider's.
- **Signature feature:** a **crimson translucent abdomen** that reads as a bead
  of blood (bright red core, darker rim), paired with a tiny high tuft/collar of
  dark scales for a caped-villain vibe. The proboscis is a glossy black fang.
- **Object list + placement:**
  - Proboscis: thick glossy near-black needle, sharp white tip glint (the fang).
  - Eye: deep blood-red compound eye with a cold white catchlight.
  - Thorax: dark hunched hump with a small ruff of raised scales (collar).
  - Abdomen: swollen, glossy crimson gradient with dark banding lines — a
    "full" belly; the horror beat.
  - Legs: six long crooked jointed legs, held in a wide dangly spider stance.
  - Wing: smoky dark-red translucent blade.
- **Palette:** `#1A0A0E` (blackberry base), `#6E0F1C` (dark blood), `#D81E34`
  (bright blood core), `#E8B8BE` (glint/highlight), `#3A2A2E` (grey scale ruff).
- **Distinctness:** the gothic/horror personality — swollen red belly + fang,
  the only one built around menace and the "hunched biter" pose.

## 5. PIP THE SKEETER  ·  `skin_mosquito_cutie` — RANK 5
- **Tier:** late-game.
- **Hero silhouette:** chibi mosquito — big round head/eye, tiny plump body,
  a soft stubby proboscis and short springy legs; reads friendly at 40px while
  keeping the needle-and-legs tell.
- **Signature feature:** an oversized **single glossy cartoon eye** (huge
  highlight) + rosy cheek blush; the proboscis is a cute rounded straw, not a
  weapon. Pastel candy palette.
- **Object list + placement:**
  - Eye: one enormous rounded eye, dark pupil + big white gleam, sitting high.
  - Proboscis: short soft tapered straw with a rounded tip, pointing forward.
  - Cheek: small pink blush oval.
  - Thorax/abdomen: rounded mint body with two chunky friendly stripes.
  - Legs: six short springy bent legs with tiny rounded feet, dangling.
  - Wing: pale rounded translucent leaf-shape (softer than the sharp adult wings).
- **Palette:** `#3B4A52` (soft charcoal outline), `#7FE3C4` (mint body),
  `#FFE08A` (belly stripe), `#FF9DC0` (cheek/pink), `#FFFFFF` (eye gleam).
- **Distinctness:** the only wholesome/kawaii take — rounds off every spiky
  feature into candy shapes so it charms instead of bites.

---

## Ranking rationale
1. **MIDNIGHT WHINE** — the definitive default; best pure-mosquito read + an
   affordable shimmer hook. Ship as the base skin.
2. **AMBER RELIC** — strongest **legendary showpiece**: baked glow + bubbles is
   the flex, and the fossil concept is genuinely novel for a bug skin.
3. **NEON SKEETER** — highest-energy crowd-pleaser; the glowing legs/proboscis
   turn the mosquito's weak silhouette parts into the selling point.
4. **VAMPING FANG** — strong themed personality; slightly busier read, so ranks
   below the cleaner three.
5. **PIP THE SKEETER** — safe charm play; broadest appeal but least surprising.

**Best legendary:** AMBER RELIC. **Safest everyday hero:** MIDNIGHT WHINE.

All five foreground the proboscis + dangling six-leg rig, so every one still
says "mosquito" at 40px before color or theme registers.
