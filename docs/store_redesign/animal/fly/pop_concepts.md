# POP FLY — Colour Variant Concepts

Five slicker, richer takes on the pop-art fly formula (Design 5 baseline).
All share: 2px #111111 ink outline on every element, Ben-Day dot fills,
two big compound eyes (with ink gutter), chunky barrel body, short wide
rounded wings, sponge labellum, 2–3 black setae on thorax hump.

**Scratch builders:** `tools/fly_candidates/pop_v{1..5}.py`
**Render sheets:** `docs/store_redesign/animal/fly/pop_v{1..5}/`

---

## 1. ELECTRIC ZAPPER

*Electric blue + neon lime — like a neon sign crossed with a comic panel.*

- **Thorax:** electric blue `#0057FF`, abdomen neon lime `#AAFF00`
- **Body Ben-Day dots:** yellow `#FFE500` halftone shadow on the abdomen side
- **Eyes:** cool white `#E8F4FF` domes, blue-dot fill `#0057FF`, white glint wedge
- **Wings:** light cyan `#C8F0FF` membrane, lime Ben-Day dots `#AAFF00`, 2 bold veins
- **Labellum:** round `#0044CC` pad
- **Ink:** `#111111` uniform 2px everywhere
- **Palette:** `#111111` · `#0057FF` · `#AAFF00` · `#FFE500` · `#C8F0FF`

---

## 2. MIDNIGHT NOIR

*Monochrome — jet black, stark white, mid-grey. Maximum graphic contrast.*

- **Thorax:** jet black `#111111` (no fill needed — just the ink outline silhouette)
- **Abdomen:** dark charcoal `#2A2A2A` dome
- **Body Ben-Day dots:** white `#FFFFFF` polka shadow
- **Eyes:** white `#FFFFFF` domes, grey-dot fill `#888888`, single black pupil slit, black glint
- **Wings:** mid-grey `#CCCCCC` membrane, white Ben-Day dots, 2 black veins
- **Labellum:** white `#FFFFFF` rounded pad, black outline
- **Ink:** `#111111` — the whole identity
- **Palette:** `#111111` · `#2A2A2A` · `#FFFFFF` · `#888888` · `#CCCCCC`
