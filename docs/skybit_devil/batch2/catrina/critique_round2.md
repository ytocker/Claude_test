# Catrina — round 2 critique (AD, final)

The plume fix landed the big one: the 4-plume bouquet now breaks the brim
outline and the silhouette reads "plumed couture hat," NOT "sombrero disc."
The Mariachi-sibling gate that blocked round 1 is cleared. But the GD's own
flag is real and now the dominant defect: the gown pinches so hard under the
brim that the silhouette nearly severs into two pieces — a floating hat and a
detached body. That breaks the single-creature read at gap scale and gates
ship. One more pass to repair the connection and Catrina is done.

## Strongest aspect
The hat. The 4-plume bouquet — tallest at back, fanning up-and-out past the
brim edge — is exactly the brief: it adds the targeted ~40% bounding height,
the upswept/tilted brim (cavalier dip) kills the flat-platter read, and the
1px silhouette alone now says "plumed hat." Side-by-side with Mariachi at 32px
the two are no longer twins: Catrina is a tall asymmetric plume-crowned mass,
Mariachi a flat horizontal disc. Palette stays dead-on the couture-festive pin
(ash-white / marigold / hot-pink / teal). Marigolds demoted to a side hatband
as directed. The make-or-break is solved.

## Weakest aspect (the new gate)
The gown-to-brim CONNECTION. Below the brim the neck/collar necks down to a
sliver — at 32px (and in the silhouette chip) the body reads as pinched-off,
so the eye sees a hat hovering above a separate skeleton rather than one
figure. A couture hourglass needs a waist, but the SHOULDER/collar line under
the brim must stay broad enough to visibly carry the hat. Right now the
narrowest point sits directly under the brim — the worst place for it — and
the thin teal column there is the same width as the parasol pole in the next
panel, which reads as accidental disconnect, not elegant taper. This is the
only thing between this and SHIP.

## Ranked fixes for the LAST round-3 pass

1. **Re-anchor the gown to the brim — widen the shoulders, drop the pinch.**
   Move the silhouette's narrowest point DOWN to the true waist (mid-gown),
   and broaden the collar/shoulder mass directly under the brim so there's an
   unbroken visual column from hat to body. Target: at 32px the hat-to-body
   join is one continuous silhouette with no daylight gap and no sub-pole-width
   necking. Keep the hourglass — just put the cinch at the waist, not the neck.
   This is the priority above all others.

2. **Give the high collar real width.** A flared couture collar (a small
   triad-lit fan or shawl at the shoulders) does double duty: it's period-
   correct Catrina AND it physically bridges the skull/brim to the gown so the
   connection can't read as severed. Make the collar the widest point of the
   upper body.

3. **Verify the 32px and silhouette chips post-fix, in that order.** The
   silhouette chip is the truth test — if hat and body are one black mass with
   a clear waist, you're shipped. Re-confirm the hat still dominates (wider/
   taller than gown) after the shoulders widen; don't let the body bulk up
   enough to rival the brim.

4. **Minor — protect the face tell at shrink.** The flower-socket eyes +
   stitched smile still soften by 48px. Hold them to 2–3 bold petal points per
   socket and a single high-contrast stitch line so the sugar-skull read
   survives. (Carried from round 1; close but not fully resolved.)

5. **Minor — parasol/pillar are good, leave them.** The scalloped ribbed
   canopy with the pointed ferrule now reads unmistakably "parasol," not dome/
   sombrero/coin, in both the prop and the gap mirror. No further work; don't
   regress these chasing the gown fix.

## Accessibility note

gown) carries the read hue-independently — good. But a severed silhouette
defeats it: a colorblind player at gap scale must see ONE figure. Fix #1
restores the connected shape tell and closes this at the same time.
